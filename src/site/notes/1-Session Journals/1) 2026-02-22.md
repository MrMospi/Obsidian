# Session Recap — Duskwatch

## Year 3434

The year was **3434**.

In the southern reaches of Sulimar stood the town of Duskwatch, a small port town on the edge of a great forest.

And on what might otherwise have been an unremarkable summer day, four travelers whose lives had very little to do with one another began crossing paths.

The first was a High Elf named **Cliovara Aurelith**.

![ClioAtDuskwatch.png](/img/user/z_Assets/ClioAtDuskwatch.png)

Clio had arrived in Duskwatch alone, another stop on a journey that had already carried her far from home.

An inquisitive young woman, Clio possessed an almost insatiable curiosity about the world around her. Wherever her travels took her, she made a habit of recording what she saw, documenting the people she encountered and the curious circumstances in which she found herself.

It wasn't long after her arrival that something considerably more interesting passed through town.

A prison wagon.

And inside were two prisoners.

One was a dwarf named **Linley**.

The other was a pixie named **Needle**.

At the time, neither had much reason to believe the passing elf would become particularly important to them.

That was about to change.

![PrisonCart.png](/img/user/z_Assets/PrisonCart.png)

---

## Out of the Somn

Another stranger arrived in Duskwatch by a considerably stranger route.

From the **The Great Forest of Somn** emerged a silver dragonborn.

His name was **Stillness**.

![OutOfTheWoods.png](/img/user/z_Assets/OutOfTheWoods.png)

Where exactly he had come from, and how he had found himself here, were questions Stillness himself couldn't adequately answer.

For the moment, however, there were more immediate problems.

His arrival drew the attention of the guards escorting the prisoners through Duskwatch.

The encounter deteriorated quickly.

The guards came dangerously close to striking the unfamiliar dragonborn before Clio intervened on his behalf.

Whatever had brought this enormous stranger out of the forest, she saw little justification for the treatment he was receiving.

Stillness required little further assistance.

Whatever the guards had expected from him, the enormous silver dragonborn proved sufficiently intimidating that the confrontation ended without them successfully detaining him.

And while the guards had been occupied with the mysterious dragonborn and the elf who had intervened for him, the situation surrounding their other charges had begun deteriorating as well.

---

## The Prisoners

Linley was able to secure a key ring through the bars off of the distracted guard and then unlocked the locked lantern containing a very angry pixie barbarian just before slipping out of his own prison.

Needle exploded out of the lantern and attacked his captors, grabbing the face of the larger guardsman and beating him unconscious.

The remaining guard abandoned the prison wagon and started to run and during all of the ensuing violence, the prison wagon carrying Linley's belongings got away.

Whatever orderly plans the guards of Duskwatch might have had for their prisoners were rapidly becoming irrelevant.

Linley made a mad dash after the run away wagon, showcasing the legendary sprinting power of the Dwarves in addition to their restriction of it only being over short distances, collapsing in an exhausted heap.

Needle, having finished beating the large prison guard looked around with feral alertness.

And Clio?

Clio was taking notes.

Having followed the prisoners and witnessed the spectacular deterioration of their imprisonment, the young elf was feverishly recording everything she could about the incident.

A mysterious dragonborn emerging from the forest. An escaping dwarf. A remarkably violent pixie.

It was certainly more interesting than anything else she'd encountered in Duskwatch.

Rather than distancing herself from the commotion, Clio continued investigating the extraordinary collection of strangers now assembled before her.

Needle, in particular, had captured her attention.

The pixie had demonstrated a remarkable capacity for violence, but Clio discovered that there were considerably gentler ways to engage with him.

She drew his portrait.

And not some hurried, crude approximation of the little fellow, either.

Clio produced a gorgeous, lifelike illustration of Needle, capturing his likeness with enough care and detail that the pixie was absolutely delighted.

He loved it.

![Drawing.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Drawing.png)

More importantly, the drawing accomplished something that the guards had failed rather spectacularly to do.

**It calmed him down.**

For all his apparent ferocity, Needle was evidently susceptible to a little artistic appreciation.

It was an unexpectedly charming beginning to their acquaintance.

And with the immediate excitement subsiding, Clio found herself accompanying Needle and Stillness through Duskwatch.

Their destination was a tavern to settle down from the afternoon's excitement.

---

## The Greyfoam Roost

The establishment was called the **Greyfoam Roost**.

Its proprietor was **Tallion Briarwisp**, a half-fey tavernkeeper who would leave one particularly enduring impression upon those who met him:

*Tallion Briarwisp was an absolute thief.*

Whatever hospitality the Greyfoam Roost might have offered, its proprietor apparently had a rather enthusiastic understanding of how much that hospitality ought to cost.

Nevertheless, the tavern provided a welcome change of scenery.

For the first time that day, these strangers could occupy the same room without guards, prison wagons, or immediate violence determining the terms of their 
conversation.

Clio had followed Needle and Stillness inside.

Linley was not with them.

Not yet.

But the dwarf wasn't far behind.

Needle, meanwhile, was making himself comfortable.

Having gone from imprisoned pixie to free patron in remarkably little time, he settled into the establishment's offerings and finished a tankard, and then another and 

then attempted to wrestle the final tankard away from Stillness and was somehow successful.

Rather than start a new confrontation with the one foot tall menace, Stillness ordered new drinks from Tallion.

![3more.png](/img/user/z_Assets/3more.png)

Considering the events of the afternoon, he'd certainly earned himself a drink.

It was around this time that Linley finally arrived.

And unlike the others, the dwarf had not come merely to enjoy the hospitality.

He had a problem.

---

## “My Wagon”

There was still one significant problem left unresolved from the chaos outside.

The prison wagon along with all of Linley's possessions was still gone.

And according to Linley, that wagon was his.

Its disappearance had left him without any proper equipment or gold, and he was now looking for assistance.

Fortunately for Linley, he had found himself in the company of a particularly curious High Elf.

Clio was interested in helping.

But not without something in return.

The elf had her own questions about the world, and one subject in particular had caught her interest.

**Fel'Garrak.** The great Dwarven capital city from which Linley hailed from.

Clio wanted to learn more about the place, and Linley's predicament presented an opportunity to arrange a mutually beneficial exchange.

She would help the dwarf retrieve his wagon.

In return, she wanted information about Fel'Garrak.

It was a straightforward proposition.

There remained, however, the small matter of assembling a party capable of retrieving the missing wagon.

Fortunately, Clio had already taken care of that.

Or, at least, she decided she had.

Having known her new companions for only a matter of hours, Clio promptly volunteered Needle and Stillness to accompany them.

It was an impressive degree of confidence to place in three people who had begun the day as complete strangers.

Still, whatever their individual reasons, the arrangement held.

The missing wagon provided an immediate objective. The prospect of learning more about Fel'Garrak gave Clio a reason to pursue it. And Needle and Stillness, each

finding themselves free of the circumstances that had brought them into conflict with the prison wagon guards, now had somewhere to go.

Four people who had begun the day with very little in common had arrived at something resembling a common purpose.

Not friendship.

Not yet.

Perhaps not even trust.

But a destination.

Somewhere beyond Duskwatch was a missing wagon carrying gear and gold.

Linley wanted it back.

Clio wanted answers.

And the other two had, somehow, found themselves recruited into the enterprise.

**And so the first journey of Clio, Linley, Needle, and Stillness began.**
