# Session Recap — Duskwatch

## Year 3434

The summer of **3434** found the small port town of Duskwatch enjoying what might otherwise have been an unremarkable day. Nestled along the southern reaches of Sulimar, where the settled world met the sprawling wilderness of the Great Forest of Somn, it was an unlikely place for the beginning of an extraordinary journey.

Among those passing through town was a young High Elf named **Cliovara Aurelith**.

![ClioAtDuskwatch.png](/img/user/z_Assets/ClioAtDuskwatch.png)

Clio had arrived alone, another stop on a journey that had already carried her far from home. An inquisitive traveler with an insatiable appetite for knowledge, she had taken to documenting her experiences in meticulous detail. People, places, customs, and curiosities all found their way into her growing collection of observations and illustrations.

That afternoon, her attention was drawn to a prison wagon passing through Duskwatch.

Its occupants were an unusual pair: **Linley**, a dwarf whose belongings had been confiscated along with his freedom, and **Needle**, a tiny pixie barbarian imprisoned within a locked lantern.

Neither seemed particularly pleased with their accommodations, though Needle's disposition suggested that the guards had good reason to keep him contained.

![PrisonCart.png](/img/user/z_Assets/PrisonCart.png)

As Clio watched the procession, another stranger arrived in town, and the guards soon found themselves with considerably more to worry about than their prisoners.

## Out of the Somn

From the depths of the **Great Forest of Somn** emerged an enormous silver dragonborn.

His name was **Stillness**.

![OutOfTheWoods.png](/img/user/z_Assets/OutOfTheWoods.png)

The circumstances of his arrival were peculiar, not least because Stillness himself could offer little explanation for them. He had come from the forest, but where he had been before that, or how he had found his way to Duskwatch, remained beyond his recollection.

His imposing appearance soon attracted the attention of the guards accompanying the prison wagon.

The soldiers regarded the unfamiliar dragonborn with suspicion, and their confrontation quickly grew hostile. Whatever answers they demanded, Stillness was in no position to provide them. The exchange threatened to turn violent when Clio, who had been observing the commotion, stepped forward in his defense.

She had no particular reason to trust the stranger, but neither did she see much justification for the treatment he was receiving.

The guards were not especially receptive to her interference. Fortunately, Stillness's considerable size and intimidating presence gave them reason to reconsider whether provoking him was worth the trouble.

With their attention fixed upon the elf and dragonborn, however, the soldiers had neglected a rather important responsibility.

Their prisoners.

## The Prisoners

Linley had been watching the confrontation with considerably more practical interests in mind.

While one of the guards was distracted, the dwarf reached through the bars of his prison and managed to lift a ring of keys from his captor.

It was an impressive bit of opportunism, though Linley chose not to use his newfound advantage exclusively for himself.

He unlocked Needle's lantern first.

The pixie needed no encouragement.

Bursting from his confinement in a frenzy, Needle launched himself at the nearest guardsman, seized the much larger man by the face, and began beating him with astonishing ferocity.

There was something deeply undignified about being assaulted by a creature barely a foot tall. There was considerably less humor in the situation for the unfortunate guardsman, who soon found himself unconscious beneath the pixie's furious onslaught.

His remaining companion took one look at the proceedings and fled.

Linley, meanwhile, had managed to free himself, only to discover that the prison wagon containing his possessions had gotten away amid the chaos.

He immediately gave chase.

For one brief, glorious moment, the dwarf demonstrated the legendary explosive speed of his people. His legs carried him down the road in a tremendous burst of effort, closing the distance with admirable determination.

Unfortunately, the legendary endurance of dwarven sprinters proved considerably less impressive.

His pursuit ended in an exhausted collapse.

While Linley struggled to recover from his athletic endeavors, Needle surveyed the aftermath of his escape with the feral alertness of a creature still perfectly willing to fight anyone foolish enough to approach him.

Clio, by contrast, was having a wonderful time.

Her notebook had come out somewhere during the confrontation, and she was now feverishly documenting everything she had witnessed. An enormous dragonborn with no memory of his arrival, a dwarf who had engineered his own escape, and a pixie who had just beaten a fully grown guardsman senseless.

There was quite a lot to record.

Of the three, Needle seemed to fascinate her most.

His tiny stature was at extraordinary odds with his temperament, and his willingness to resort to violence had made approaching him a somewhat delicate proposition.

Clio, however, had an idea.

Rather than attempting to restrain or reason with the furious little barbarian, she set about drawing his portrait.

The result was exquisite. With a careful eye and considerable artistic talent, she produced a remarkably lifelike illustration of Needle, capturing the little pixie's appearance in gorgeous detail.

Needle was delighted.

![Drawing.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Drawing.png)

The transformation was almost immediate. His aggression gave way to an enthusiastic appreciation of the artwork, and Clio found herself in the rather extraordinary position of having pacified a violent barbarian with a drawing.

It was an unconventional introduction, but an effective one.

With the guards thoroughly routed and the immediate excitement subsiding, Clio accompanied Stillness and Needle through Duskwatch in search of somewhere to recover from the afternoon's events.

A tavern seemed an appropriate destination.

## The Greyfoam Roost

The establishment they found was called the **Greyfoam Roost**, a local tavern operated by a half-fey named **Tallion Briarwisp**.

Tallion was a man of considerable entrepreneurial spirit, though those unfortunate enough to do business with him might have described his talents somewhat differently.

_Tallion Briarwisp was an absolute thief._

Nevertheless, the tavern offered food, drink, and the welcome prospect of an evening without further interference from the town guard.

Needle wasted little time making himself comfortable.

Having spent the afternoon imprisoned in a lantern and subsequently engaged in a rather strenuous bout of violence, the pixie seemed determined to enjoy the freedoms of his new circumstances.

He began with a tankard of ale.

Then another.

Neither proved sufficient.

When Needle had exhausted his own supply, his attention turned toward the drink belonging to Stillness.

The enormous dragonborn had scarcely any reason to expect that his tankard would become the subject of a physical contest with a one-foot-tall pixie, but Needle apparently regarded the matter as an entirely reasonable challenge.

He wrestled Stillness for the ale.

And won.

Somehow.

Stillness, faced with the prospect of escalating a dispute over a drink with the tiny barbarian, decided that ordering another round would be considerably less troublesome.

![3more.png](/img/user/z_Assets/3more.png)

Clio continued observing her new acquaintances, occasionally returning to her notes as the evening unfolded. The contrast between the taciturn dragonborn and the irrepressible pixie provided no shortage of entertainment.

Their unusual gathering was soon joined by another familiar face.

Linley had finally caught up with them.

The dwarf's failed pursuit had left him in a difficult position. His freedom was secure, but his equipment, gold, and other belongings were still aboard the missing prison wagon.

And he had every intention of retrieving them.

## "My Wagon"

Linley explained his predicament to the others, hoping to enlist their assistance in tracking down his property.

Clio listened with interest.

The dwarf had already demonstrated a remarkable talent for escaping imprisonment, even if his attempts at long-distance pursuit left something to be desired. More importantly, there was something about Linley himself that had captured her curiosity.

He hailed from **Fel'Garrak**, the great dwarven capital.

Clio had heard of the city, and the prospect of learning more about it was too tempting to ignore. Here, sitting across from her, was someone who might be able to answer her questions about a place she had yet to visit.

She proposed an arrangement.

Clio would help Linley recover his belongings, and in return, the dwarf would tell her more about Fel'Garrak.

For Linley, who had spent much of the afternoon trying unsuccessfully to recover his possessions, the offer was welcome. For Clio, it was an opportunity to satisfy her curiosity while lending assistance to someone in need.

The two came to an agreement.

There remained the question of who would accompany them.

Clio had an answer to that as well.

With the same enthusiasm she had demonstrated throughout the afternoon, the young elf promptly volunteered her new friends, Needle and Stillness, to join the expedition.

It was an ambitious decision, considering she had known the two for only a matter of hours, and neither had been especially involved in negotiating the arrangement.

Nevertheless, the plan was settled.

By the end of their evening at the Greyfoam Roost, the day's peculiar encounters had produced an equally peculiar traveling party: an inquisitive High Elf, an exceptionally violent pixie, a silver dragonborn with a missing past, and a dwarf whose worldly possessions were currently somewhere down the road.

They had little experience traveling together, and even less understanding of what might await them beyond Duskwatch.

But Linley had a lead to pursue, Clio had questions she wanted answered, and Needle and Stillness had somehow been recruited into helping them both.

**Their first journey together would begin the following morning.**
