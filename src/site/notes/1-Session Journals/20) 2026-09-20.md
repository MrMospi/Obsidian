# Canon Session Recap

## The Storm Remembers

Night settled over Lowshield, but sleep did not come easily to Needle.

The storm outside **The Sheltered Tide** had grown violent. Wind battered the building. Rain hammered against the walls and windows. Thunder rolled across the coast and shook the ancient stones of Giant's Grasp.

And with every peal, Needle hurt.

The headaches that had plagued him since arriving in Lowshield had become something more. The storm seemed to wrack his entire body with pain, each flash of lightning and roar of thunder pulling against something buried deep inside him.

Needle did not remember this storm.

But his body did.

Every crack of thunder made something behind his eyes tighten. Every gust against the building felt like the beginning of a thought he couldn't finish.

He could wait.

Morning would come. The storm would pass. Whatever stood atop Giant's Grasp would still be there.

But somewhere beneath the headache, beneath thought, Needle knew something with absolute certainty.

> **If you wait until morning, it won't be the same.**

Needle got out of bed.

And of everyone sleeping around him, he woke **Surang**.

He told him that he needed to go.

Now.

Surang listened.

Soon Clio was awake as well, and a considerably less enthusiastic Linley was dragged from his sleep in a groggy, grumpy state.

Together, the four of them stepped out into the storm.

## Giant's Grasp

The ancient stones loomed over Lowshield.

In daylight, Giant's Grasp had been enormous. In the storm, it seemed almost incomprehensible.

Visibility was terrible. Rain swept sideways across the harbor, and the massive stone fingers disappeared periodically into darkness. Only lightning revealed the scale of what lay ahead.

Ancient rope bridges stretched between the stones, swaying violently in the wind.

The party began crossing.

Every step became a contest against the storm. They hauled themselves across the bridges through wind and rain, relying on strength and agility to keep themselves from being thrown into the darkness below.

The second bridge had been damaged.

Needle was nearly swept away.

He caught hold of a rope and clung desperately to it as the remains of the bridge bucked beneath him.

Linley forced his way across and took hold of the rope Needle was hanging from, keeping him from being torn away.

The party still needed to restore the crossing.

Using arrows that would not be deterred by the terrible weather, they fired a line attached to the damaged bridge rope across to Surang. He managed to retrieve it and draw the broken ends back together.

Clio reached the torn rope.

With a spell, she **Mended** it.

The bridge pulled itself back together.

With the crossing restored, Clio and Surang were able to make their way safely to the far side.

Eventually, all four reached the enormous pillar Bram had described.

Somewhere above them was the supposed shrine.

They looked upward.

Needle had to climb.

Clio would follow him.

Surang and Linley remained below.

## The First Memory

Needle began climbing.

Rain streamed down the pillar. Wind tore at his wings and clothing. The stone beneath his hands was slick and cold.

Then a gust struck him.

And for just an instant—

Giant's Grasp was gone.

> You're smaller.
> 
> Much smaller.
> 
> Something enormous is reaching toward you.
> 
> A hand.
> 
> Fingers close around your entire body.
> 
> You remember being helpless inside them.
> 
> You remember the giant lifting you.
> 
> And then—
> 
> Nothing.
> 
> Rain.
> 
> Stone.
> 
> Your hands are gripping Giant's Grasp again.

Below him, Clio could only watch.

Needle had stopped moving.

For a moment he simply gripped the stone, frozen against the pillar and staring at something that wasn't there.

Then he began climbing again.

## Shelter

Needle reached a small plateau in the stone.

For a moment, the shape of Giant's Grasp protected him from the worst of the wind.

The storm disappeared.

Not silence.

**Shelter.**

And another memory broke through.

> You're somewhere small.
> 
> Walls surround you.
> 
> A giant is crouched outside.
> 
> Something enormous moves across the opening.
> 
> You remember being enclosed.
> 
> You remember something being placed over you.
> 
> You remember—
> 
> warmth.
> 
> The memory recoils from itself.
> 
> Because you remember the walls.
> 
> You remember the giant.
> 
> You remember being unable to leave.
> 
> But you cannot make the fear fit.
> 
> You were warm.
> 
> You were safe.
> 
> And then the storm screams back into your ears.

Needle continued upward.

Clio followed behind him, but the climb was becoming increasingly treacherous.

Her footing slipped.

She caught herself before she fell, but recovering cost her precious time.

By the time she regained her position, Needle had disappeared into the storm above.

## The Hand

Needle climbed alone.

The pain behind his eyes had become nearly unbearable.

Another step.

Another.

Lightning tore across the sky.

And suddenly he was standing somewhere else.

No—

**Here.**

> You're standing here.
> 
> Giant's Grasp beneath your feet.
> 
> But you're smaller.
> 
> The storm is enormous.
> 
> Someone is shouting behind you.
> 
> You turn.
> 
> A giant is running toward you.
> 
> You remember the wind catching beneath your wings.
> 
> You remember leaving the stone.
> 
> You remember falling.
> 
> And the hand—
> 
> That enormous hand from before—
> 
> reaches for you.
> 
> Fingers spread.
> 
> Desperate.
> 
> Almost—
> 
> Almost—

Needle fought through the agony splitting his head.

And this time, the memory did not escape him.

He saw it.

> You remember the hand missing you.
> 
> You remember the sound that came from somewhere above as you fell.
> 
> Not anger.
> 
> Not command.
> 
> **Fear.**
> 
> Then the memory breaks.

Needle opened his eyes.

The stone was beneath his hands.

The summit was above him.

He climbed.

## The Shrine

Needle's hand reached over the final ledge.

Stone.

He pulled himself upward.

And for the first time since leaving The Sheltered Tide, the wind began to die.

Not all at once.

The storm seemed to spend itself around him.

Rain softened against the stone.

Thunder rolled farther out to sea.

And above the clouds, somewhere beyond the black, the first break in the sky appeared.

Before Needle stood the shrine.

He entered alone.

Inside were remnants.

Small things.

Familiar things.

Fragments of a place he could not completely remember, yet somehow knew.

There was a stone bed.

Whatever had once made it soft and comfortable was long gone. Nothing remained but bare stone.

Needle climbed into it anyway.

It was comfortable.

He lay there.

And for the first time that night, Needle slept.

## Clio Reaches the Summit

Eventually, Clio pulled herself over the final ledge.

The storm had begun to break around her as well.

She entered the structure and found Needle inside.

Sleeping.

Peacefully.

Clio did not wake him.

Instead, she took out her drawing materials.

She drew the strange little shrine atop Giant's Grasp.

She drew its shape.

Its construction.

And the enormous symbol carved into the stone above it.

Eventually, Needle woke.

He stepped outside with Clio.

Lowshield had changed.

## Family

The rain had stopped.

Water still ran in narrow streams down Giant's Grasp, falling hundreds of feet toward the sea below.

Behind them, pale light finally broke through the clouds.

As it passed across the ancient stone, something caught it.

The carving Clio had noticed earlier.

During the storm, rain had filled its grooves faster than either of them could properly examine it.

Now the water drained away.

The symbol became clear.

Needle looked at it.

And recognized it.

He didn't know how he recognized it.

He simply did.

The meaning that first came to him was:

> **OURS.**

But even that wasn't quite right.

There was a word in Common that felt closer.

A word that carried what the symbol actually meant.

> **FAMILY.**

![NeedleHome 1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/NeedleHome%201.png)

And then Needle felt something else.

Something inside him had changed.

There had been a hollow place inside him for longer than he could remember.

Something had been clawed out of him.

And something else had been burning in the hole it left behind.

He had carried that fire so long that he had stopped recognizing it as something foreign.

But now—

It went out.

No pain.

No struggle.

It simply had nowhere left to burn.

Because the hollow wasn't empty anymore.

Something was rushing back into it.

Not everything.

Not enough to make the years whole.

Shapes.

Voices.

Hands.

Laughter.

Rain.

A home built impossibly small by people impossibly large.

Memories came cascading back to Needle—broken, incomplete, overlapping—

**but his.**

And for the first time since he had lost them, the thing filling that space inside him...

**was him.**

Something new awakened within Needle.

When he finally left the summit, he did so under his own power.

Needle spread his wings and flew down toward Surang and Linley.

Leaving Clio to make the entire descent by herself.

## Beyond Lowshield

Before beginning that long descent, Clio stopped and looked outward from the summit of Giant's Grasp.

From this height, she could see farther than she ever had from Lowshield below.

The **Glasslands** stretched away into the distance.

Vast.

The storm that had torn through Lowshield seemed to have been drawn toward the desert, where another storm raged endlessly across the horizon.

Farther north, Clio could make out the distant shapes of what could only be **Il'Madris**.

She recorded what she could see.

One detail in particular complicated the party's plans.

The river they had considered following north through the Glasslands was dry.

Whatever water once flowed from the desert was no longer reaching Lowshield.

Clio eventually made her way back down Giant's Grasp.

The party returned to town.

## A Word from Madagan

Madagan and the crew of the **Pampered Pig** were preparing to depart.

Madagan himself was carrying an entire platter of breakwater cakes beneath one arm while simultaneously attempting to eat several of them.

He casually asked whether the party would be making their way toward Il'Madris.

They corrected him.

They intended to enter the Glasslands.

Madagan stopped.

For once, the manic First Mate became completely serious.

He placed a hand on Surang's shoulder.

> **“Lad, we've been through a lot together…”**
> 
> **“…and I like ya, so bend yer ear a little.”**
> 
> **“Ain't my business to stop yous if yer set on goin.”**
> 
> **“But that desert is a death sentence.”**

Madagan looked down at the map.

> **“And from here?”**
> 
> **“You're walking into the longest stretch of it.”**

![Advice1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Advice1.png)
![Advice2.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Advice2.png)

Clio confirmed what he was saying.

She had seen the sheer distance from atop Giant's Grasp.

Their alternative idea of following the river was no better. The river flowing out of the Glasslands had dried up.

Crossing the entire expanse from Lowshield would mean attempting the longest possible stretch of desert without reliable access to food or water.

The party reconsidered.

They would go to **Il'Madris**.

From there, they could enter the Glasslands much closer to its midpoint.

And so, once again, the party boarded the Pampered Pig.

The crew were delighted to see Clio return.

## Back Aboard the Pampered Pig

During the voyage, Clio took the last of the enchanted thread she had received from Seris.

She worked some of it carefully into **Linley's cloak**.

The remainder went into **Needle's Murk pelt**.

Both now carried the thread's glamour.

Linley, meanwhile, remained concerned about their destination.

He questioned Madagan about the current situation in Il'Madris and whether they might be sailing directly into an active war zone.

Madagan couldn't give him a firm answer.

He had been away too long to know precisely what awaited them.

Needle found another way to occupy himself.

He returned to the captain's quarters.

This time, without permission.

Needle searched Carnach's old desk.

Every drawer was locked.

Almost every drawer remained that way.

Needle barely managed to open one.

Inside, he found a small silver dagger—small, at least, by the standards of a normal-sized person. A red gemstone was set into its hilt, resembling the eye of a dragon.

Needle took the dagger.

Everything else remained exactly where he had found it.

Elsewhere aboard the ship, Surang began a project of his own.

Using a javelin and several pieces of wood he had accumulated during the party's travels, he began constructing a staff capable of carrying Needle's lantern.

After roughly half a day's sailing, the great port city finally appeared ahead.

**Il'Madris.**

## A Delivery for the Baron

The Pampered Pig docked.

Almost immediately, Clio informed Madagan that she would very much like to accompany him when he visited **Baron Horlutz**.

Surang understood immediately.

Clio wanted to become captain of the Pampered Pig.

Madagan agreed to let the party help bring Horlutz's sample goods up from the ship.

That agreement lasted approximately as long as it took Madagan to realize he could make the party do the entire job.

> **“Toodles! Say hi to the Baron for me!”**

Madagan remained behind with the ship.

The party carried the samples themselves.

Their route took them upward through Il'Madris, following a winding hill toward a secluded estate overlooking the city.

They knocked.

The door was answered by **Tartha Grey**, Baron Horlutz's housekeeper and maid.

Tartha regarded the unexpected group skeptically.

Eventually, however, she agreed to bring them inside.

And the party finally met the mysterious Baron Horlutz.

He was not what they expected.

The imposing trade lord they had heard about was a tiny, elderly red dragonborn with a cane, a terrible disposition, and approximately all the warmth of an old cactus.

He demanded to see the samples.

## Ghastly

Horlutz stood beside the newly arrived stack of imported cloth, inspecting each piece with the grim severity of a magistrate sentencing criminals.

Tartha waited nearby with a ledger tucked beneath one arm.

Horlutz reached the final bolt.

It was beautiful.

Fine dark green silk, richly woven, its color shifting subtly wherever the light caught it.

Horlutz stared at it for several seconds with increasing displeasure.

Then he lifted one corner between two claws.

> **“Ghastly.”**

Tartha looked from him to the fabric.

> **“Baron, this is one of the better pieces.”**

> **“Precisely.”**

He let it fall back onto the table.

> **“Unsellable. Offensive to the eye. Remove it.”**

![Ghastly1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Ghastly1.png)

Tartha paused.

> **“Are you certain, Baron? It's freshly imported.”**

> **“I don't care. Burn it. Bury it. Throw it into the harbor.”**

Tartha carefully gathered the silk and began folding it.

> **“Very good, sir. I shall make it my highest priority to remove this item.”**

Horlutz gave a brusque little grumble and reached for his tea.

> **“See that you do.”**

He took the cup.

Then, with studied indifference:

> **“Perhaps it might find a modicum of redemption in one of your little women's hobbies.”**

Tartha stopped folding for just a moment.

Then she smiled warmly, clearly ecstatic despite every effort at decorum.

She smoothed the silk once more.

> **“If this table rag has any hope at all, I'll find it, Baron.”**

Horlutz raised the cup to his mouth.

And took a sip.

A considerably longer sip than necessary.

Just long enough to hide the smirk.

## Captain Clio

Clio could contain herself no longer.

She immediately launched into her proposal.

She wanted to become captain of the Pampered Pig.

![TheCaptain2.png](/img/user/z_Assets/TheCaptain2.png)

After all, they knew the original captain.

And **Carnach was dead**.

Horlutz changed.

The declaration visibly shook him.

He demanded to know who these people were.

Surang spoke up.

He had known Carnach.

Carnach had been his mentor.

Horlutz stared at him.

> **“…dead then?.....greedy bastard.”**

Then:

> **“Mentor you said?”**

A pause.

> **“Wait here.”**

The old dragonborn toddled away.

Eventually he returned carrying a document.

It was the original contract through which Horlutz had hired the **Iron Gull Company** for the mercenary work that had first brought Carnach into his employment.

He handed it to Surang.

> **“Here, not exactly a keepsake but it's something.”**

Horlutz returned to his chair, noticeably quieter than before.

> **“This is no place for such a discussion...”**

Then he looked toward Tartha.

> **“Tartha, I'll be going out this evening, do see to that overabundance of pork left by that delivery boy, I can't stand the smell of decaying pork.”**

Needle's attention snapped toward him.

> **“Pork you say?”**

Horlutz stared incredulously at the pixie.

> **“Tartha, prepare a plate for this filthy rat boy.”**

Tartha did.

Then she prepared food for everyone else as well.

Much to the Baron's loudly feigned displeasure.

## One Dreadful Day

Horlutz watched the party eat.

> **“Well, you've delivered both terrible goods and terrible news. Are there any other ways you would like to ruin my day? Apart from devouring my entire food stock that is.”**

Surang considered the question.

> **“I could arm wrestle you if you'd like.”**

Horlutz stared at him.

> **“Ah yes, physical harm. The perfect final cherry on a dreadful day.”**

Then, without hesitation:

> **“Tartha will arm wrestle you.”**


![armwrestle.png](/img/user/z_Assets/armwrestle.png)


Surang accepted.

He put up an impressive effort.

Tartha beat him anyway.

Clio, meanwhile, had not forgotten the actual reason she wanted to meet Horlutz.

She once again asked about becoming captain of the Pampered Pig.

Horlutz rolled his eyes.

He was apparently unwilling to entertain the farce of a vacant captaincy any longer.

> **“Madagan is captain of that ship whether he chooses to acknowledge the title or not.”**

Nor did Horlutz see any reason to belabor Madagan's apprehension toward using the title so long as the man remained competently employable.

Clio was slightly deflated.

Only slightly.

## The Tam Lin of Fortitude

Clio raised another matter.

The party had come from Farhaven.

They were friends with **Councilman Harrow Blackwall**.

Horlutz looked more closely at her.

The glamour concealed much, but not enough.

He discerned that Clio was a High Elf and immediately questioned the legitimacy of her claim.

Clio convinced him that she was telling the truth.

Horlutz reluctantly accepted it.

He also confirmed what the party had feared.

Il'Madris was sitting firmly within the Queen's grasp.

And there was a **Tam Lin** within the city.

Only one, as far as Horlutz knew.

Clio produced the pages stolen from the Thurandil archive.

Horlutz examined them.

The Tam Lin currently occupying Il'Madris was the **Tam Lin of Fortitude**.

She had been ushered into the city by **Lord Howe**, who had done so in his attempts to curry favor with the Queen.

Horlutz eventually announced that he intended to freshen up.

The party could return for him that evening.

They would be accompanying him on his outing.

For delivering the sample goods, Horlutz paid them **100 gold**.

He also instructed them to take the entire shipment to his warehouse despite the apparently catastrophic quality of its contents.

## Tartha's Request

The party prepared to leave.

Before they could, Tartha approached them privately.

Her concern was genuine.

Despite the way Horlutz acted, she explained, he was an incredibly caring person.

And he was living in a city with a shrinking number of allies.

She asked the party to take care of him.

There was one additional warning.

They should not allow him to drink too much.

> **“He's a real bastard when he drinks sometimes.”**

Surang promised that he would watch over Horlutz and bring him home safely.

The party departed the estate.

## The Under-Quay

They returned to the docks.

Madagan was already pacing, waiting for them.

The moment he saw the group:

> **“Well? What'd he say?”**

The party explained that Horlutz had concluded the samples were terrible.

They were to be delivered to his warehouse anyway.

Madagan brightened.

> **“Okeydokey! Figured as much!”**

Clio then began making **extremely heavy suggestions** that certain changes may have occurred regarding the captaincy of the Pampered Pig.

Madagan willfully ignored every one of them.

Checking the water level, Madagan noted that low tide was approaching.

That meant the best taverns and bars in Il'Madris would soon become accessible.

Beneath the visible city lay the **Under-Quay**, Il'Madris's underbelly, hidden among the submerged foundations and reachable only when the tide withdrew.

Madagan and the crew left to deliver Horlutz's shipment to the warehouse.

The party had time before they were expected to retrieve the Baron.

So they explored.

The presence of Thurandil was obvious throughout the central portions of Il'Madris.

The party relied on their glamoured clothing and did their best not to attract unwanted attention.

Eventually, evening drew nearer.

They turned back toward the winding road leading up to Horlutz's estate.

The Baron was waiting.

And somewhere within Il'Madris was the **Tam Lin of Fortitude**.
