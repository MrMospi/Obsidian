# Session Overview

#  Session Recap — The Lantern Festival & Escape from Farhaven

##  Merrit’s Instruments

The session begins by returning briefly to an earlier point in the Lantern Festival.

**Clio has just finished singing for Merrit Wren**, fulfilling his unusual requirement before he will introduce her to the “more shy” members of his inventory. Merrit treats the process less like selling instruments and more like matchmaking: musician and instrument must suit one another.

Clio takes this responsibility extremely seriously.

She spends considerable time inspecting, handling, and testing Merrit’s instruments, and notices something strange along the way: **the runes upon her body react more strongly to some instruments than others.**

Eventually, Clio narrows the collection down to three.

Then she asks Merrit whether she can have all three.

Merrit admits that he would not ordinarily allow such a thing. However, between Clio’s obvious enthusiasm, her performance, the apparent endorsement of her beloved bagpipes, and Merrit’s examination of the **new rune that has formed upon her arm**, he decides to make an exception.

Clio leaves with all three.

When she tests her new instruments again, her performance inspires both **Surang and Linley**, granting them Heroic Inspiration.

With that, Clio finally fulfills the requirements of her chosen feat:

###  Clio ascends to Level 4 — **Musician**

---

#  A Gift from Seris

Clio reunites with **Eltharion and Seris** to proudly show off her new instruments.

As they eventually part ways, Seris gives Clio something unusual:

**golden thread.**

Seris explains that Clio may be able to use it to reproduce something similar to the glamour Seris herself employs—magic that subtly encourages attention to slide away from her.

Exactly what Clio will do with it remains to be seen.

---

#  Surang’s Lantern

Away from the loudest portions of the Festival, **Surang finds a lantern vendor** and purchases one of his own.

Nearby, a father is preparing lanterns with his two young children.

Surang approaches to encourage them.

Initially, the enormous silver dragonborn succeeds mostly in frightening the children.

They quickly warm to him.

Together, Surang and the family release their lanterns into the night, watching the lights rise above Farhaven.

---

# Linley Listens In

Elsewhere, Linley remembers seeing **Renn Valehart and Tharim Ironquill** entertaining wealthy guests during the Festival, including members of the Fel’Garrak delegation.

He decides to investigate.

Using the enormous Festival crowd for cover, Linley slips closer unnoticed, hoping both to overhear the dwarves and perhaps finally catch sight of **Stone-Marshal Dagna Koll**.

He succeeds.

Tharim is speaking directly with Dagna in Dwarvish.

And Linley hears something alarming.

Dagna states quite openly that:

> **when Farhaven falls under Fel’Garrak’s sovereignty, they will have all the time they need to force open the Forge.**

Tharim nervously pushes back.

Such statements, he insists, are unnecessary.

She already has:

> **“all the time in the world for your work in Hamyrhal.”**

Linley leaves before anyone realizes he was listening.

Whatever Fel’Garrak ultimately intends for Hamyrhal, Dagna’s conception of Farhaven’s future is becoming increasingly difficult to ignore.

---

#  The Extraction Begins

As the Festival begins winding down, the party finally reconvenes to begin the operation they have been planning for days:

**get No-Name out of Farhaven.**

There is one immediate problem.

Nobody knows where **Needle** is.

They are out of time.

The party decides to proceed anyway and deal with whatever happens.

Clio separates from the others and heads toward the Festival stage. Her role is simple:

**perform—and keep as much of Farhaven looking in her direction as possible.**

Meanwhile, Surang and Linley take their wagon, loaded with supplies, toward the sewer entrance leading to Hamyrhal.

They wait.

The city bell rings.

According to Garr’s information, the guards should now be changing shifts.

The replacement guards never arrive.

Linley makes the call.

They cannot wait any longer.

> **Follow my lead.**

---

#  Councilor Tharim’s “Refreshments”

Linley immediately launches into an elaborate performance.

He has graciously arrived bearing refreshments and wine for the guards, supposedly courtesy of **Councilor Tharim**.

His simpleton dragonborn servant carries the wine.

Surang plays along.

![Simpleton.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Simpleton.png)

The guards do not.

Suspicious, they approach and begin drawing their weapons.

And then—

### **BOOM.**

![BadGuy.png\|500](/img/user/z_Assets/BadGuy.png)

Several fireworks explode outward from the sewer entrance.

One guard is caught by the blast and hurled violently into a wall.

Surang immediately tackles the other, landing on top of him.

Linley rushes in and begins kicking the guard in the face while shouting:

> **“GET OFF HIM, SIMPLETON!!”**

Unfortunately, Linley’s Strength modifier means that he is functionally incapable of hurting him.

Then the masterminds behind the explosion emerge.

**Needle and Trixie.**

Trixie is carrying the remains of several spent fireworks.

Needle is carrying a **half-drunk barrel of wine**.

Apparently they had been quite busy.

They help finish subduing the remaining guard.

No-Name is loaded into the wagon.

And before the city can understand what just happened, the party leaves through **Farhaven’s main gate**.

Against all reasonable expectations:

### **No-Name is out of the city.**

---

#  Needle Is Sobered

There remains another problem.

Needle is extremely drunk.

Surang solves this through healing magic, forcibly sobering the pixie.

The process works.

It also leaves Needle effectively incapacitated on the floor of the wagon.

The group continues onward into the night.

---

#  The Ash Road

![GaltrMadelayTessa.png\|800](/img/user/z_Assets/GaltrMadelayTessa.png)

Eventually, Surang notices **debris deliberately blocking the road ahead**.

He immediately suspects trouble.

Then voices call from the darkness.

> **“Leave the wagon.”**

> **“Take your weapons and walk away.”**

> **“Nobody needs to die.”**

Another assurance follows:

> **“You can retrieve the wagon in the morning.”**

Surang refuses to back down.

Clio casts **Detect Thoughts** on one of the shadowed figures.

What she discovers is not confidence.

It is panic.

Desperation.

The ambush was supposed to work immediately.

The travelers were supposed to see the weapons, surrender the wagon, and leave.

Now that Surang has refused, the people surrounding them are rapidly losing control of the situation.

Clio cheerfully reports her assessment to the others:

> **“We can take ’em!”**

---

#  “MONSTER!”

Then No-Name pokes his head out of the wagon.

A scream tears through the darkness.

> **“MONSTER!!”**

A crossbow fires.

The bolt strikes No-Name squarely in the chest.

And something happens to Needle.

---

#  MUST PROTECT FRIENDS

![NoNameInjured 1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/NoNameInjured%201.png)

In the moments after No-Name is struck, Needle’s mind is overwhelmed.

Fear.

Pain.

Rage.

Thoughts—or perhaps something less distinct than thoughts—crash through his connection with the Myconid.

> **MORE BAD ONES.**

> **NO MORE.**

> **NO MORE HURT.**

> **CRUSH THE BAD ONES.**

> **MUST PROTECT FRIENDS.**

Is it No-Name’s voice?

Needle’s?

At that moment, the distinction ceases to matter.

The tiny barbarian explodes into **Rage**.

His enormous ancestral guardians manifest around him.

And Needle launches himself into the darkness directly toward the young woman holding the crossbow.

### **Roll initiative.**

And there the session ends.

The party successfully got No-Name out of Farhaven.

Now they have to survive what protecting him has awakened.
