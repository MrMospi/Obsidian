# Session Recap — The Lantern Festival

The session resumes in **Farhaven’s Council Hall**, with the party’s plans for No-Name’s extraction rapidly approaching the point where they will actually have to attempt them.

## The Missing Mark

Before leaving, Clio decides that she absolutely must speak with Ilyra about the strange alterations that had appeared on her map.

She heads into the Archive and attempts to enter quietly.

Instead, she discovers **Ilyra Quent and Professor Baldric Garr** happily chatting together in what appears to have been a rather private moment.

Clio attempts to remain unnoticed.

She does not succeed.

Once discovered, Clio produces the map and asks about the region previously altered by the mysterious scroll—the area that had been circled, annotated, and marked with the word:

**BAD**

before that warning was later crossed out.

There is now a new problem.

Every supernatural addition to the map is gone.

The circle.

The writing.

The crossed-out warning.

All of it has vanished.

Ilyra cannot identify anything particularly notable about the location Clio indicates. Garr, however, believes there is a swamp somewhere in that region, though he knows little about it beyond that.

Not wanting to intrude any further, Clio leaves Ilyra and Garr as she found them.

A short time later, Garr comes rushing out of the Council Hall to catch the party before they leave.

He cannot provide the transportation they need for No-Name.

But he can provide something perhaps more important.

Information.

The Fel’Garrak guards stationed near the sewer entrance work predictable shifts.

Garr knows when they change.

If the party intends to move No-Name without the dwarves discovering them, Garr can help create their window.

With that promise made, he returns to the Archive.

## Caleb

![calebwhisper.png\|500](/img/user/z_Assets/calebwhisper.png)

The party next follows up on Garrick’s recommendation for someone capable of chartering them a wagon and draft animal.

They find a man named **Caleb**.

They also discover that Caleb is almost completely incomprehensible.

His accent and dialect are so tremendously thick that the party struggles to understand virtually anything he says.

Clio eventually resorts to magic.

She casts **Comprehend Languages**.

The spell works perfectly.

Unfortunately, it reveals something deeply unhelpful.

Caleb is speaking Common.

Perfectly ordinary Common.

Technically.

Unable to make meaningful progress, the party temporarily gives up and heads elsewhere.

## Tea from Hamyrhal

The party arrives at the **Wayfarer’s Refuge**, which is already overflowing with travelers, locals, and visitors preparing for the coming Lantern Festival.

After managing to flag down an extremely busy Seris, Clio acquires a kettle of hot water.

At last, the party can experiment with the mysterious tea sachets recovered from the sealed box in Hamyrhal.

Several produce unusual effects.

One, however, is considerably more significant.

### Needle’s Vision

Needle drinks.

And suddenly he is somewhere else.

Not watching.

Not imagining.

Experiencing.

He sees through the eyes of a **Fel’Hamyr dwarf** from centuries ago.

The dwarf enters the great Forge of Hamyrhal to relieve another worker from their shift.

Outside, a thunderstorm rages across the mountain.

Inside, the Forge is alive.

Operational.

And above it hangs an enormous suspended crystal.

The storm intensifies.

Far above the Forge, the great vein of **Stormsilver running through the mountain becomes charged**.

Lightning races through it.

An immense bolt travels down through the stone and discharges directly into the suspended crystal.

Then Needle awakens.

The Refuge returns.

The party is around him again.

Whatever the tea contained, it was not merely showing Needle a picture.

For a moment, he had experienced something from Hamyrhal’s past.

The group experiments with only one additional sachet before deciding that whatever remains is far too strange—and potentially far too valuable—to waste casually.

The rest of the tea stays sealed.

For now.

## Planning No-Name’s Escape

That evening, Eltharion joins the party at the Refuge.

No-Name is still hidden beneath Farhaven.

The Lantern Festival is approaching.

And the party still needs a way to move him.

Eltharion confirms that he _can_ arrange a wagon through his own contacts.

That does not mean he should.

Questions would be asked.

People would remember.

Records might be created.

And eventually, somebody might become curious about why **Eltharion Vaelis** suddenly needed a wagon capable of quietly transporting something out of Farhaven.

After considerable discussion, the party reaches a decision.

Convenience is not worth the attention.

They will find the wagon elsewhere.

With Garr’s knowledge of the dwarven guard rotations, the outline of the plan finally begins to take shape.

Use the Lantern Festival as cover.

Move No-Name during the cleanest possible guard window.

Get him out of the sewers.

Hide him in a wagon.

And get beyond Farhaven before anyone realizes he is gone.

For the first time, the rescue is beginning to resemble an actual plan.

## Garrick, Caleb & Whisper

The following morning, the party returns to **The Broken Standard**.

They need help.

Specifically, they need somebody capable of understanding Caleb.

Garrick agrees to come with them.

The party returns.

Caleb speaks.

And somehow—

inexplicably—

**Garrick understands him perfectly.**

With Garrick acting as translator, negotiations finally become possible.

The party secures a wagon.

And a draft horse.

His name is **Whisper**.

Problem solved.

Then Surang casts **Speak with Animals**.

Whisper talks.

Surang listens.

And discovers that Whisper possesses an accent so impenetrable that he can barely understand the horse either.

Apparently Caleb and Whisper are perfect for one another.

During this time, Clio lets Eltharion know that his assistance acquiring transportation is no longer necessary.

One more possible source of unwanted attention quietly disappears from the plan.

They have their wagon.

They have their horse.

Now they only have to smuggle a wounded Myconid out of Farhaven during the largest celebration the city has held in some time.

Progress.

## Clio Searches for Music

With transportation secured, the party helps Seris prepare her stand for the Festival.

Clio then turns her attention to another matter.

Her performance.

She needs an instrument.

Preferably a strange one.

Preferably immediately.

The markets are already overflowing with visitors, merchants, performers, and buskers preparing for the celebration.

Clio searches.

Eventually, she finds a musician performing in the crowded streets.

She joins in.

The result is...

fine.

Not disastrous.

Not particularly inspiring.

Fine.

The busker eventually shoos her away.

Clio moves on.

Somewhere in Farhaven, the correct instrument still waits.

Probably.

## The Lantern Festival

Eventually, the party joins the rest of Farhaven in the heart of the market square.

The city has been waiting for this.

Harrow Blackwall steps before the enormous crowd.

He speaks.

And when he finishes—

the **Lantern Festival begins**.

Farhaven erupts.

Fireworks burst above the old stone walls.

Lanterns glow along the streets.

Music pours through the market.

People dance.

Drink.

Eat.

Sing.

Laugh.

For once, the party is not being sent anywhere.

Nobody is asking them to kill anything.

There is no immediate catastrophe demanding their attention.

Instead, they are simply allowed to exist in the city they have spent so much time protecting.

They wander.

They drink.

They reconnect with the friends, merchants, guild members, councilors, allies, eccentrics, and acquaintances they have accumulated throughout their time in Farhaven.

People normally encountered during crises are simply... here.

Celebrating.

For one evening, Farhaven is not a place under siege.

Not a city waiting for the next disaster.

It is home.

## Trixie Volunteers

At some point during the celebration, Needle finds Trixie.

He has a question.

Would she be willing to help the party with something involving No-Name?

He does not explain everything.

He does not need to.

Trixie immediately confirms that she would **LOVE** to help.

Exactly what Trixie considers “help” remains to be discovered.

## Merrit Wren

Clio’s search for an instrument eventually leads her to one of the Festival’s stranger merchants.

**Merrit Wren.**

Merrit has instruments.

Many instruments.

But apparently not all of them are willing to meet just anybody.

Some of Merrit’s inventory is, as he describes it, rather shy.

Before he will introduce Clio to any of them, he needs to know something.

What kind of musician is she?

Merrit cannot simply hand her an instrument at random.

The musician and the instrument must be properly matched.

So Clio will have to perform.

Only then will Merrit decide which of his more unusual instruments might be willing to meet her.

At long last, Clio may finally have found someone who understands the assignment.

Her performance will have to wait.

Because elsewhere in the Festival, something considerably less celebratory is happening.

## Selka’s Report

Clio notices **Mistwright Selka** moving quietly along the edges of the crowd.

Curiosity wins immediately.

Clio follows.

Selka approaches Harrow away from the center of the celebration.

Clio positions herself close enough to listen.

The missing Farhaven trade caravan has been found.

It reached Il’Madris.

It simply never left.

The merchants were taken.

The teamsters.

The guards.

Every Warden assigned to protect the convoy.

All of them are in custody.

Harrow asks the obvious question.

By whose authority?

Selka answers.

**Her Majesty, Queen Elysira of Thurandil.**

The captives are believed to be alive.

Prisoners.

Not casualties.

For the moment.

But Selka has more.

The caravan is not the real scale of the problem.

Something larger is happening in Il’Madris.

Much larger.

The knights whose names have only recently begun surfacing in Eltharion’s research are no longer pieces of buried history.

> **The Tam Lin have been deployed to Il’Madris.**

Around them, the Lantern Festival continues.

Music spills through the streets.

People laugh.

Lanterns sway above the crowd.

Fireworks burst over Farhaven.

And somewhere beyond the celebration, the legendary knights of Thurandil are moving.

For the first time in the campaign—

the session ends on a cliffhanger.
