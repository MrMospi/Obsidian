# 📜 Session Recap — Blood on the Ash Road

## ⚔️ The Ambush Continues

The session resumes exactly where the previous one ended.

No-Name has revealed himself from the wagon.

A terrified young woman has screamed:

> **“MONSTER!!”**

A crossbow bolt has struck him in the chest.

And Needle’s mind has been overwhelmed by the fear and rage pouring through his connection with the Myconid:

> **MORE BAD ONES.**  
> **NO MORE.**  
> **NO MORE HURT.**  
> **CRUSH THE BAD ONES.**  
> **MUST PROTECT FRIENDS.**

Needle enters a Rage.

His enormous ancestral guardians manifest around him.

And everyone rolls initiative.

---

# No-Name’s Rage

Linley acts quickly, firing upon **Galtr** and injuring him badly enough to disarm him.

At the same time, No-Name begins flooding the battlefield with spores.

The cloud envelops Needle and empowers him, giving the pixie a strange, almost **Haste-like burst of speed and mobility**.

Surang immediately recognizes the danger.

He attempts to contain Needle before the situation can escalate further.

Needle breaks free.

And flies directly toward **Tessa**, the young woman who shot No-Name.

---

# The Ash Road Erupts

Any possibility of the confrontation remaining contained disappears.

The ambush erupts into full combat.

All but one of the people blocking the road commit themselves to the fight.

Weapons are drawn.

Magic and gunfire tear through the darkness.

Linley raises his firearm and shoots Galtr again.

The shot lands perfectly.

### **Natural 20.**

The bullet tears through Galtr’s chest, killing him instantly.

The encounter continues to spiral.

And shortly afterward, Needle reaches Tessa.

The pixie strikes her savagely in the head.

### **Tessa dies.**

---

# Too Late

Throughout the fighting, Surang continues attempting to reach No-Name.

Eventually, he succeeds.

Surang persuades the frightened Myconid that the people attacking them are **not their enemies**.

The fear begins to subside.

But by then, it is already too late.

Galtr is dead.

Tessa is dead.

And everyone on the road has seen what just happened.

---

#  Trixie

Trixie stares at the aftermath in horror.

Whatever excitement she had carried into the night is gone.

She withdraws into herself, shocked by the violence she has witnessed—and by who she has watched commit it.

Surang attempts to get everyone moving again and tries to pass responsibility for the wagon to Trixie.

She recoils.

Clio attempts to approach her.

Trixie recoils from her too.

Then she runs.

The party does not follow.

---

#  Outside Duskwatch

Surang tells the others to meet him outside **Duskwatch** while he returns the wagon.

Clio, Linley, and Needle travel ahead and make camp outside the city.

The night affects each of them differently.

**Linley sleeps easily.**

**Clio meditates uneasily.**

**Needle does not sleep.**

Meanwhile, Surang brings the wagon back to **Caleb’s stable** before immediately turning around and double-timing it toward Duskwatch.

By the time he reaches the others, their rest is coming to an end.

---

#  “FLAME PRISON BROKEN!?”

As dawn rises, No-Name sees the sun.

He panics.

To the Myconid, the brilliant light overhead appears terrifyingly familiar:

> **flame prison broken!?**

The party reassures him.

They tell him to trust them.

And together they continue toward the **Ebonmurk**.

---

#  A Home for No-Name

Eventually, the party finds a place within the swamp that seems comfortable for No-Name.

Safe.

Wet.

Alive.

Somewhere he might actually be able to build something of his own.

The party tells him:

### **This is home now.**

Clio makes a drawing for him before they leave.

Surang has one final request.

He points to No-Name’s hammer.

And offers his own war pick in exchange.

What follows is a lengthy negotiation between the paladin and the Myconid.

Eventually, No-Name agrees.

The two exchange tools.

### They trade **garden makers.**

The party says goodbye to their strange friend and begins the journey back toward Duskwatch.

---

# What Comes Next

On the road, the party begins discussing their next move.

Clio and Linley finally share information they overheard during the Lantern Festival.

They discuss **Fel’Garrak’s intentions**.

They discuss the reported deployment of the **Tam Lin**.

And after considering the increasingly dangerous situation surrounding Il’Madris, the party reaches a decision:

### They need to reach the Glasslands as quickly as possible.

And, if they can manage it:

### **They do not want to travel through Il’Madris.**

Their preferred route becomes reaching **Lowshield by sea**.

---

#  Duskwatch

The party returns to Duskwatch and books a room at the inn before beginning their search for passage.

At the docks, the consequences of the situation in Il’Madris are already visible.

Several trade groups appear to be held up by the newly reported difficulties surrounding the city.

With sea travel looking uncertain, the party begins discussing an alternative route:

travel north through part of the Somn toward **Clear Rest**.

Then a wiry old man sidles up beside Linley.

> **“HEH?! LOWSHIELD YE SAY???”**

A grin.

> **“I can get ye ta LOWSHIELD heh heh heh!”**

---

#  First Mate Madagan

The old sailor introduces himself as **First Mate Madagan**.

And immediately makes one thing exceptionally clear:

> **“NOT the captain!”**

His ship has a captain.

And Madagan is **not him**.

Only the captain himself—or the owner of the ship—could make Madagan captain.

The ship’s owner, he explains, is a man named:

### **Baron Horlutz.**

The name catches Clio’s attention.

She knows she has heard it before.

But she cannot immediately remember where.

Then the party asks the obvious question.

If Madagan is only the First Mate...

Who is the captain?

Madagan answers.

The captain of the ship was a man named:

### **Carnach.**

For Surang, the world seems to fall away beneath his feet.

---

#  Dinner with “Mad” Madagan

The party invites Madagan to join them for dinner and drinks at the inn.

He agrees with extraordinary enthusiasm, nearly sprinting ahead of them.

Over food and drinks, the party begins asking questions.

Madagan tells them what he knows about:

- **Baron Horlutz**
    
- the current political situation in **Il’Madris**
    
- the whereabouts and status of **Lord Howe**
    
- and what kind of place **Lowshield** is
    

Madagan is extremely cooperative.

Whether he is equally helpful is another question.

His answers frequently disappear into manic ramblings and enthusiastic tangents.

It is also increasingly difficult to determine whether Madagan is actually drunk.

Eventually, however, an agreement is reached.

### At first light, Madagan will take them to Lowshield.

---

#  Horlutz and Farhaven

Later, Clio finally remembers where she heard the name.

During the Lantern Festival, she had overheard **Harrow speaking with Selka about getting a message to Horlutz**.

That connection changes the party’s understanding of their new contact.

If Harrow trusted Baron Horlutz enough to reach out to him during the growing crisis...

then Horlutz may be an ally of Farhaven.

---

#  Linley Ascends

Linley reflects on the battle against the refugees.

Despite how brutally the encounter ended, he is pleased with his own performance and the effectiveness of his growing skillset.

While resting, something settles into place.

###  Linley ascends to Level 4.

His chosen feat:

### **Skulker**

---

#  The Hammer

While Linley continues drinking with Madagan, Surang finally tells Clio why he was so interested in No-Name’s hammer.

He shows her the map.

He points out the **Fel’Hamyr clan symbol**.

And explains what he believes the hammer may represent.

![LinSecret1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/LinSecret1.png)

Clio’s jaw drops.

Her immediate response:

> **“We can’t tell Linley.”**

At that exact moment, Linley walks back into the room.

> **“Can’t tell Linley what??”**

Linley attempts to figure out what they are hiding.

![LinSecret2.png](/img/user/z_Assets/LinSecret2.png)

### **Natural 1.**

Whatever Surang and Clio know remains between them.

For now.

---

#  Garden Maker

Later, Surang attempts to sleep.

He feels the subtle pull of an object asking to be understood.

The hammer requires **attunement**.

Surang takes it out and begins the process.

As the connection forms, fragments of the object’s history flow into him.

He learns its proper name:

# **Forgewright’s Charge**

An ancient Fel’Hamyr tool.

Not originally a weapon.

But the hammer has another name.

One it has answered to for a very long time.

A name given to it by the friend who carried it into the darkness beneath Hamyrhal.

### **Garden Maker.**

Surang is given the choice.

He could call it by the name of the Forgewrights who created it.

Or by the name No-Name gave it.

Surang chooses:

# **Garden Maker.**

And there the session ends.
