# Session Recap — The Green Door

## A Light at the Gate

We rejoin the party in the streets of **Il’Madris**.

Evening has settled over the great port city as Surang, Clio, Linley, and Needle make their way through the twisting streets and narrow alleys leading back toward Baron Horlutz's estate.

Needle, apparently deciding that simply knocking on the door would be insufficient, flies to the top of the estate gate.

There, he begins flashing his pixie light.

A signal.

Presumably.

Unfortunately, nobody at the estate appears to notice.

More unfortunately, none of the party understands the code.

Most unfortunately, neither does Needle.

He flashes harder.

Still nothing.

Eventually, Needle abandons the operation.

The party knocks.

Tartha Grey answers and welcomes them back, explaining that the Baron is preparing for their evening outing.

Or, perhaps, has nodded off in his chair.

She goes to fetch him.

Before long, Baron Horlutz appears.

The elderly dragonborn takes up his cane and begins leading the party toward their destination at a truly breathtaking pace.

> **“Keep up, youngin's!”**

And so begins the journey.

## A Night on the Town

Horlutz leads them through many of the same back alleys the party had used earlier in the day.

Slowly.

Very slowly.

Occasionally a drunken resident of Il'Madris lies unconscious in their path.

Horlutz does not go around them.

His cane comes down.

> **“Move.”**

Eventually, the party realizes that Horlutz seems to be retracing their steps.

Then they reach a dead end.

Then another.

They cross the market square, where Thurandil soldiers remain stationed. The party moves cautiously through, their glamours helping them avoid unwanted attention.

Then another alley.

Another turn.

Another dead end.

Concern begins to set in.

Perhaps the Baron doesn't actually know where they're going.

Perhaps they are going to wander Il'Madris behind this tiny old dragonborn for the remainder of their natural lives.

Finally, Horlutz turns down another narrow alley.

Brick wall.

Dead end.

Horlutz stops.

> **“Ah! Here we are!”**

He raises his cane.

And raps it against the brick wall.

## The Green Door

There is a door.

A **green door**.

A door where, moments before, there had been nothing but brick.

More troubling still, there does not appear to be enough space beyond the wall for an establishment of any meaningful size.

Horlutz seems entirely unconcerned.

The door opens.

A remarkably handsome man stands on the other side, immaculately dressed and possessed of manners even finer than his clothing.

His face brightens.

> **“Baron! It's been too long!”**

Horlutz grunts.

> **“Lovelace. Oh, and these are some associates of mine.”**

The man turns his attention toward the party.

> **“Very good! I am Lovelace.”**

He gestures inside.

> **“Welcome, friends, to The Green Door!”**

The party enters.

And finds themselves somewhere unlike anywhere they have ever been.

## A Different Kind of Tavern

The Green Door bears little resemblance to the taverns of Sulimar.

Its furnishings are strange. Its conveniences unfamiliar. Its style seems to belong to another place—or perhaps another time entirely.

A curious machine sits in one corner.

From it comes music.

Clio immediately forgets almost everything else in the room.

The machine becomes the object of her complete fascination.

A small metal arm touches a flat black circle as it turns, somehow producing music. Lifting the arm stops it. Removing the black circle also stops the music.

Clio begins investigating.

![Gramophone1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Gramophone1.png)
![Gramophone2.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Gramophone2.png)

Meanwhile, Lovelace guides the party toward a comfortable lounging area and takes orders for drinks and food.

![KissTheChef.png\|500](/img/user/z_Assets/KissTheChef.png)
![KissTheChef2.png\|500](/img/user/z_Assets/KissTheChef2.png)

Linley settles in remarkably well.

The beverages are bizarre.

He likes them.

More surprisingly, he finds himself enjoying Horlutz's company.

Needle does not share his enthusiasm.

From the moment he entered, something about The Green Door has felt wrong.

## Carnach

Once everyone is settled, Horlutz turns toward Surang.

There is something he wants to know.

What happened to **Carnach** after the man left his employment?

Surang tells him.

Carnach eventually found his way to the village of **Scales**.

There he built a life.

He trained the village's egglings—including Surang and his sister—in weapons and martial combat. And while he trained them, he filled their heads with stories of all the places he had traveled and the things he had seen before settling among them.

Surang remembers his own aptitude proudly.

> **“I was always gifted with natural ability and Carnach trained me to become the best fighter in my village.”**

Horlutz looks at him in mock astonishment.

> **“Really?! The best in your whole village!”**

He gives Surang a look.

> **“...rather cocky aren't you, boy?”**

Conversation moves on.

Horlutz takes another drink.

And then, quietly:

> **“You remind me of him.”**

## A Loveless Being

Eventually, conversation turns toward the strange proprietor himself.

Someone asks Lovelace how he came to know Baron Horlutz.

Lovelace seems happy to answer.

> **“There was a time, not so very long ago, when I encountered our dear Baron by chance. Happenstance, I assure you.”**

His smile remains pleasant.

> **“He found my manner... wanting.”**

Naturally, someone asks the obvious question.

What did Horlutz say?

> **“Many things. Several of them considerably unkind.”**

Lovelace smiles faintly.

> **“But among the kinder accusations, he called me a loveless being.”**

A moment passes.

> **“I have spent the years since determining whether his unkindness was also untrue.”**

## Something Is Wrong

Clio continues investigating the gramophone.

Linley continues drinking.

Horlutz continues being Horlutz.

Needle watches.

Lovelace can seemingly be anywhere in the establishment almost instantaneously.

The room itself doesn't make sense.

The objects don't make sense.

And beyond the main room, Needle notices something else.

A hallway.

A light curtain hangs across its entrance.

Needle moves closer.

Beyond the curtain, the hallway stretches away into darkness.

And keeps going.

Doors line its walls.

Other darkened passages branch away from it.

There is no visible end.

Needle turns invisible.

And enters.

He doesn't get far.

Something catches him by the wings.

Lovelace.

The proprietor addresses Needle as though the invisibility isn't there at all.

> **“Do forgive me, however I must insist that you do not venture down that way without a guide.”**

His tone remains perfectly courteous.

> **“It would be quite disastrous if you were to become lost.”**

Then, pleasantly:

> **“Is there anything at all I might help you find?”**

Needle says nothing.

He pulls himself free.

His invisibility disappears.

He returns to the others.

And glares at Lovelace.

## “Isn't It Great?”

Needle leans toward Clio.

Something is wrong with this place.

Clio looks around enthusiastically.

> **“I know! Isn't it great?!”**

She is still holding the strange black disk.

Needle insists.

So Clio casts **Detect Magic**.

Her senses ignite.

Magic.

Everywhere.

Everything.

Too much.

But worse than the sheer amount is how unfamiliar it feels. This is not magic as Clio understands it.

Whatever she is perceiving, her senses have no comfortable way to make sense of it.

The sensation becomes unbearable.

Clio drops the spell.

Horlutz watches her skeptically.

> **“Are you having a fit, young lady?”**

Clio recovers.

And realizes that she has the Baron's attention.

There is, therefore, important business to discuss.

## Captain Cliovara Aurelith

Clio wants the **Pampered Pig**.

Or, more specifically, she wants to become its captain.

Again.

Surang positions himself on one side of Horlutz.

Linley takes the other.

Surang offers his counsel.

> **“Honestly Baron, she's never going to stop. It would be faster and easier for everyone if you just made her captain.”**

Linley has a different perspective.

> **“Can you believe the AUDACITY?! Such entitlement is hardly a surprise from an elf, as I'm sure you well know, Baron.”**

![TheCaptain3.png\|800](/img/user/z_Assets/TheCaptain3.png)

Clio glowers at him.

But this time, beneath the ridiculousness of the campaign for captaincy, Clio explains why she wants it.

She has wandered.

She has traveled.

And through all of it, she has never found a place or purpose that felt like home in quite the same way as the Pampered Pig.

She isn't asking to immediately replace Madagan.

Madagan can continue leading the ship and its crew as First Mate—

> **“Which I believe would be acceptable to him!!”**

—while Clio learns how to properly inherit the responsibilities of captaincy.

And she promises to uphold Horlutz's legacy.

Horlutz scoffs.

> **“Legacy? Bah.”**

He is conducting a business.

Not a sightseeing tour.

He would just as soon ensure his affairs are properly in order before donating his only ship to some elf's whimsical adventuring.

Then, smugly:

> **“After all, it's not like I have any family to pass my belongings on to anyway.”**

Clio answers immediately.

> **“What about Tartha?”**

Horlutz's expression changes.

> **“Careful, girl.”**

There is venom in the warning.

Clio does not push further.

But she does not abandon her argument.

And eventually, Horlutz concedes.

Not the ship.

Not yet.

A candidacy.

There will be conditions.

Clio will **learn maritime**, so that she does not become a burden aboard the vessel.

She will **continue to represent Horlutz's interests**.

And someday, should Clio meet someone whose obvious enthusiasm rivals—or even exceeds—her own...

She will train that person to become captain.

Agree to those terms, Horlutz tells her, and he will consider her candidacy more seriously.

Clio agrees immediately.

Of course she does.

## Carnach's Knife

While the negotiations have unfolded, Needle has been entertaining himself with something else.

The silver dagger he stole from the captain's quarters aboard the Pampered Pig.

Eventually, the movement catches Horlutz's eye.

> **“Little rat boy, may I see that?”**

Needle reluctantly hands it over.

Horlutz examines the dagger.

Recognition settles across his face.

> **“I haven't seen this in a very long time.”**

He turns toward Surang.

> **“Do you know what this is?”**

It belonged to Carnach.

One of the ridiculous hidden daggers Carnach used to conceal behind his eyepatch.

An utterly insane method, in Horlutz's estimation.

Apparently quite effective despite being completely bat-shit crazy.

He never thought he would see the blade again.

Horlutz does not hand it back to Needle.

He presents Carnach's dagger to **Surang**.

Needle watches.

And something in him finally snaps.

## “I NEED ANSWERS NOW.”

Needle moves in a flash.

He catches Horlutz's hand.

Slams it against the bar.

A knife comes down beside it.

> **“I NEED ANSWERS NOW.”**

The room changes.

> **“WHO ARE YOU AND WHAT IS THIS PLACE?”**

Needle stares at Lovelace.

> **“WHAT ARE YOU HIDING?!”**

Linley moves immediately.

His gun comes out.

It points at Needle.

> **“LET HIM GO.”**

Lovelace is already there.

The easy composure he has carried throughout the evening diminishes.

And somehow, the entire bar seems to diminish with it.

His hands remain raised.

Nonaggressive.

Controlled.

> **“Please, there's no need for this.”**

![Hiding1.png\|500](/img/user/z_Assets/Hiding1.png)
Needle doesn't relent.

> **“Who are you?!”**

He demands to know what this place is.

What Lovelace is hiding down that hallway.

Lovelace answers.

Calmly.

He snaps his fingers.

The curtain moves aside.

For the first time, the entire party can see what Needle saw.

The hallway.

Stretching onward.

And onward.

And onward.

Doors disappearing into impossible distance.

Darkened branches leading away from it.

No end.

> **“I am not hiding anything.”**

Lovelace reminds Needle that he had already explained himself.

He stopped Needle because becoming lost down there would be disastrous without a guide.
![Hiding2.png\|500](/img/user/z_Assets/Hiding2.png)
Then:

> **“...and I am not allowed to interfere.”**

![Hiding3.png\|500](/img/user/z_Assets/Hiding3.png)

Needle falters.

He releases Horlutz.

Then, furious, he throws his knife down the hallway.

It disappears into the darkness.

They wait for the sound of it landing.

Nothing comes.

## An Attempt on the Baron

Clio immediately checks Horlutz.

> **“Are you alright, Baron?”**

Horlutz gruffly waves away the concern.

> **“Yes yes, I'm fine.”**

He glares at Needle.

> **“Wouldn't be much of a Baron without the occasional attempt on my life, would I?”**

Surang has seen enough.

> **“I think it would be best if we go now...”**

Nobody argues.

The evening at The Green Door has come to an end.

## The Door Remains Open

As they prepare to leave, Lovelace's familiar warmth returns as though nothing has happened.

He bids the party farewell.

> **“Should you so desire, my door remains open to you!”**

Before Surang leaves, Lovelace presents him with a calling card.

It is neatly embossed.

There is no address.

No directions.

Nothing except three words:

> **THE GREEN DOOR**

The party begins filing outside.

Needle follows.

But Lovelace stops him.

> **“Sir?”**

Needle turns.

Lovelace reaches into his breast pocket.

> **“I believe you may have dropped this.”**

He produces the knife.

The same knife Needle threw down the endless hallway.

The knife that vanished into darkness without ever making a sound.

Needle looks at it.

Then at Lovelace.

For perhaps the first time that evening, Needle seems genuinely impressed.

Instead of taking it back, he returns the knife to Lovelace's hand.

Lovelace considers the gesture.

His smile returns.

> **“A souvenir then?”**

He slips the knife neatly into his breast pocket.

> **“Very good sir!”**

Needle leaves.

The party turns around.

There is no Green Door.

There is no establishment.

Only the alley.

Only brick.

As though nothing had ever been there.

## Home, Baron

The party retraces its long journey through Il'Madris.

Eventually they return to Horlutz's estate.

The Baron has had quite enough to drink.

Surang places a hand on him and channels healing magic, clearing the alcohol from his system.

Horlutz is suddenly, completely, and involuntarily sober.

He is not pleased.

The party bids farewell to one very disgruntled Baron Horlutz.

And their first visit to **The Green Door** comes to an end.

## The Best Drinking Spot in Il’Madris

After watching Horlutz safely enter his home, the party turns back toward the city.

The night is not over yet.

There is still one destination they have been told they absolutely must see.

In Madagan's own words:

> **“The best drinking spot in all of Il’Madris!”**

They make their way downhill toward the docks.

But when they arrive, the waterfront has changed.

The tide has gone out.

The water level has fallen dramatically, and several of the ships that had been moored alongside the docks have drifted backward with the retreating sea.

In their absence, more of Il’Madris has appeared.

Architecture that had previously been swallowed beneath the water now stands exposed.

Stonework.

Foundations.

And a great dark archway beneath the docks.

There are no stairs leading down.

No ladder.

No obvious means of descent at all.

The opening below is dark and deeply unwelcoming.

Needle offers the only immediately practical solution.

He can carry them.

## Beneath the Docks

Needle takes Clio first.

Carefully—as carefully as someone can while simultaneously exerting every ounce of strength in his tiny body—he carries her down toward the exposed stone archway.

Eventually they find what they are looking for.

A narrow walkway runs through the tunnel within, its stone slick with sea grime.

Clio finds her footing and lights a lantern.

Needle flies back for Linley.

Standing within the tunnel, Clio can see plainly that this is not a place that belongs to the people of Il’Madris.

Not permanently.

The ocean claims dominion here.

The wet stone and accumulated grime make clear that everything around her will disappear beneath the water again when the tide returns.

For now, however, a path stretches onward into the darkness.

Needle soon returns.

With Linley.

A very much **flailing** Linley.

He deposits the dwarf safely beside Clio.

But he does not return for Surang.

Surang has decided not to come.

## All Cargo Lost

Surang remains alone on the docks.

The others descend into the strange underbelly of Il’Madris while he watches the dark water and the ships beyond.

His thoughts remain elsewhere.

With Carnach.

The evening with Horlutz has given him glimpses of a man he knew for much of his life—but a version of that man Surang never knew at all.

Carnach before Scales.

Carnach before the egglings.

Carnach before Surang.

And in Surang's possession is something else Horlutz gave him.

The original contract.

The first time Baron Horlutz ever hired Carnach and the **Iron Gull Company**.

Surang reads.

It is painfully bureaucratic.

An expedition in **Veyrasa**.

Protection for a trade caravan.

The results of the expedition are recorded in dry black ink.

> **All cargo lost.**

> **Zero casualties.**

> **Payment rendered in full.**

Nothing sentimental.

Nothing grand.

Just the record of a job.

And laid out in that painfully bureaucratic black ink are the foundations of what would eventually become a lifelong friendship between Baron Horlutz and Carnach.

Surang remains on the docks.

And reads.

## What Tide Are You Coming Back On?

Below, Clio, Linley, and Needle continue into the darkness.

Their lantern illuminates the wet stone ahead as they follow the passage farther beneath the great port city.

Eventually, the tunnel ends at a large portcullis and an equally sea-battered door.

They knock.

Nothing happens immediately.

Then a small opening in the door slides aside.

A voice comes from the other side.

> **“What tide are you coming back on?”**

Clio answers.

> **“We're in from Lowshield, off the Pampered Pig.”**

A pause.

Then:

> **“The Pig? You here with Madagan?”**

Clio enthusiastically confirms that they are.

There is an obvious groan from the other side of the door.

Then the locks move.

The door opens.

And Clio, Linley, and Needle take their first steps into the **Under-Quay**.

## The Under-Quay

A vast domed cistern opens before them.

Water fills much of the chamber, longboats drifting across it and disappearing into channels leading deeper beneath Il’Madris.

Makeshift shops crowd the perimeter.

Lanterns burn throughout the cavernous space.

Voices carry over the water.

People move through the night.

Above them, Il’Madris had been watched by Thurandil soldiers.

Its streets had required caution.

Its market square had made the party nervous simply to cross.

Down here, another Il’Madris is awake.

Alive.

Noisy.

Crowded.

And very much enjoying itself.

A cry immediately rises above the surrounding commotion.

> **“Roasssssted lizzzzards, get your roasssssted lizzzzzards here!”**

![Ssssam.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Ssssam.png)

Needle bolts toward the food.

The vendor selling the roasted lizards is, somewhat bizarrely, himself a lizard-man.

This does not deter Needle.

He purchases some.

Once food has been acquired and the immediate crisis resolved, the party asks the vendor whether he knows where they can find Madagan.

He points them farther down one of the channels.

Clio, Linley, and Needle continue deeper into the Under-Quay.

![Under-Quay1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Under-Quay1.png)

And eventually, through all the voices, drinking, music, and general frivolity—

They hear one voice they know extremely well.

## Fancy Seeing You Lot Here

They follow the sound.

Before long, they find the crew of the **Pampered Pig** enjoying their night in the Under-Quay.

And there, naturally, is Madagan.

He looks up.

> **“Oh hello again! Fancy seeing you lot here!”**

![The Under-Keg.png](/img/user/z_Assets/The%20Under-Keg.png)

The crew looks over.

Then they see Clio.

And immediately light up.

The night in Il’Madris, apparently, is only just getting started.
