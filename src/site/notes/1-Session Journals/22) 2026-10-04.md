# Session Recap — Payment Rendered in Full

## Black and White

Above the hidden revelry of the Under-Quay, Surang remained alone on the docks.

He looked again over the old contract Baron Horlutz had given him—the first record of Carnach and the Iron Gull Company entering the Baron's employ.

The document remained maddeningly terse.

>**All cargo lost.**  
>**Zero casualties.**  
>**Payment rendered in full.**

Whatever had happened on that expedition, the contract wasn't going to tell him. Eventually Surang decided there was only one person who could.

He would ask Horlutz the next time he saw him.

Returning to the Pampered Pig, Surang discovered that the entire crew had apparently abandoned ship for the Under-Quay—with one exception.

Gaff, the half-orc sailor, had been left behind to keep watch.

Surang offered to take over.

Gaff required remarkably little convincing.

Before disappearing into the night, he left Surang with one piece of valuable local knowledge:

> **“If you're looking for a good time, Ruby's is the best.”**

Surang made a loose mental note of advice he was almost certainly going to ignore.

With the Pig now quiet, he decided to look around.

---

## What Carnach Left Behind

Surang returned to the captain's quarters.

Needle's earlier incursion had left both the door and the desk drawer unlocked. Inside remained the belongings of the man Surang had known as his mentor—the remnants of an entire life Carnach had lived before coming to Scales.

Surang searched.

The desk offered nothing unexpected. Neither did the floorboards.

Then he examined the old sea chest containing Carnach's clothes.

The bottom was false.

Beneath it, Surang discovered a hidden map.

Several small expeditions had been marked leading into the **Somn**. Others traveled northeast into the mountains.

Surang knew that region.

On Torvald's map, those mountains bore a considerably less precise annotation:

**HERE BE DRAGONS.**

It was also the region Surang suspected contained his home.

**Scales.**

Surang took the map, carefully replaced the false bottom of Carnach's chest, and returned to his watch.

---

## The Returning Tide

Far beneath him, the evening in the Under-Quay was finally winding down.

Clio, Linley, and Needle remained with Madagan and the crew as people began boarding the longboats that traveled the channels beneath Il'Madris.

Then a great gong sounded throughout the cistern.

Madagan took that as his cue.

> **“Another successful night out me boyos! Time we be shovin' off!”**

The great portcullis connecting the cistern to the ocean opened. Water levels began to equalize, and one by one the boats of the Under-Quay sailed back out toward the harbor.

From the deck of the Pampered Pig, Surang watched them emerge.

Eventually he spotted his companions among them.

And one extremely drunk elf spotted him.

> **“Surang! Hiiiiiiiii!”**

Clio had returned.

---

## A Night Aboard the Pig

The party settled aboard the ship for the remainder of the night.

Clio found a place to meditate near the prow, while Surang slept as nearby as he reasonably could—given that members of the crew continued discovering an astonishing number of chores that just happened to require their presence somewhere in Clio's vicinity.

Linley headed below deck and found himself a comfortable place among several sacks of potatoes.

He took eight.

He stuffed them into his shirt.

No further explanation was offered or required.

Needle attempted once again to sleep in the crow's nest.

Sleep found him.

Unfortunately, so did the dream.

He was back in the woods, chasing the other redheaded pixie. Once again she remained ahead of him, crying out as he desperately tried to reach her.

But this time, among her cries, Needle understood something new.

One word.

> **“Brother.”**

Needle pushed after her.

And woke falling out of the crow's nest.

His wings caught him before gravity could finish the job.

The remainder of the night brought little meaningful rest.

---

## Suddenly Very Busy

Morning came aboard the Pampered Pig.

Clio attempted to corner Madagan.

After everything that had happened with Horlutz, she finally had news concerning her campaign for the captaincy.

Madagan was—

**SUDDENLY VERY BUSY.**

Terribly busy.

Unavoidably busy.

He would absolutely catch up with her later.

As the party prepared to leave the ship and begin gathering supplies for their expedition into the Glasslands, however, they discovered someone waiting for them on the dock.

**Tartha Grey.**

Tartha thanked them for returning the Baron:

> **“In better shape than when he left, despite his insistence otherwise.”**

Curiously, Horlutz had apparently said very little about the evening.

Tartha then turned to Surang.

She remembered his offer to assist with **“disposing of the unwanted pork.”**

If the offer still stood, she could use his help later that afternoon.

Surang agreed.

For now, the party had shopping to do.

---

## Apothecary Jeremiah Neare

Il'Madris's market square was already bustling.

Thurandil soldiers remained stationed throughout it, but between the crowds, the party's anonymity, and their glamoured cloaks, they felt reasonably confident moving through the market.

Their first destination was an apothecary.

The shop belonged to **Jeremiah Neare**.

Inside, dim shelves were crowded with flasks and phials containing liquids of every description. Some held strange objects suspended within them. Others contained things that looked disturbingly like creatures.

Clio tapped the side of one flask containing something resembling a newt.

It flailed uselessly.

Then became still.

A husky whisper came from behind them.

> **“What... can I help you find?”**

![Jeremiah Neare.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Jeremiah%20Neare.png)

Jeremiah Neare was pale and unsettlingly thin, with cloudy yellow eyes, long stringy hair, and a mask pulled high across the bridge of his nose.

Nothing in his shop appeared to be labeled.

When the party asked what he sold, Jeremiah answered simply:

> **“I am certain I can conjure whatever you might be seeking... so tell me... what are you seeking?”**

Needle didn't hesitate.

> **“STRENGTH.”**

Jeremiah stared at him.

Then disappeared among the shelves.

In his absence, Clio quietly voiced a concern.

> **“I don't like this. This guy gives me the creeps.”**

Linley was appalled.

> **“OH, JUDGING A BOOK BY ITS COVER. JUST LIKE AN ELF.”**

Surang decided to settle the question another way.

He reached outward with his divine senses.

Jeremiah Neare was **undead**.

---

## Strength

Jeremiah eventually returned carrying two small flasks filled with vibrant red liquid.

Needle proposed a deal.

Let him sample one. If it worked, he'd purchase the other.

Jeremiah agreed.

Needle drank.

The results were immediate.

His tiny body swelled with tremendous strength, his musculature expanding so dramatically that portions of his clothing—including his loincloth—simply tore away.

Clio immediately repaired the damage with Mending.

A low, husky laugh came from behind Jeremiah's mask.

> **“...the effects will persist through the day... until your next long rest.”**

Linley wondered whether the effects could be prolonged.

Jeremiah returned with a syringe containing some other substance.

And, perhaps jokingly, perhaps not, a cup of coffee.

Clio intervened.

> **“No! I mean no thank you... I think Needle will be perfectly happy with the potion's intended duration.”**

Jeremiah shrugged.

He pocketed the syringe.

Then pulled down his mask.

He had **no lower jaw**.

Jeremiah poured the coffee directly down his open throat, replaced his mask, and continued as though nothing unusual had occurred.

Needle was delighted with his purchase.

He decided the two potions were worth forty gold and handed over the coins.

Jeremiah accepted them.

Then continued staring at Needle.

> **“Not... enough.”**

Before anyone could meaningfully object, Jeremiah grabbed the pixie, carried him to the counter, positioned a container beneath him, and repeatedly smacked Needle's bottom until a satisfactory quantity of pixie dust had fallen into it.

Then he dropped Needle onto the floor.

![PixieDust.png](/img/user/z_Assets/PixieDust.png)

Linley grinned.

> **“I like him.”**

Jeremiah examined his newly acquired dust.

> **“Payment rendered... in full. May I help you find....anything else?”**

Clio decided they had concluded their business.

> **“No thank you, I think we have all we need for now.”**

Linley spoke over her.

> **“We will CERTAINLY be back soon!”**

Jeremiah disappeared into the rear of the shop.

Outside, Clio quietly told Surang:

> **“I don't trust it, and I can only IMAGINE what he would want from the rest of us in exchange for his goods.”**

Surang considered this.

A very reasonable concern.

---

## Kaeporah Gaeborah

Clio's next priority was considerably safer.

Books.

There was no obvious bookshop in the square, but a tall, crooked spire caught her attention. Around its base stood carts filled with books and scrolls.

Perched atop a nearby lamppost was an extremely old-looking owl.

Surang gently stroked the bird, waking him from his daze.

The owl looked at them pleasantly.

> **“Oh hello there. My name is Kaeporah Gaeborah. What may I assist you all in finding?”**

Clio asked whether he possessed anything concerning the Glasslands or **Eothar's Bane**.

Kaeporah flew into one of the spire's high windows and eventually returned with a tiny roll of parchment.

He offered to read what little information he possessed.

Then began reciting the familiar legend of Eothar.

And continued.

And continued.

And continued.

The amount of information leaving the owl bore no conceivable relationship to the amount of parchment he was holding.

![storytime.png](/img/user/z_Assets/storytime.png)

Several eternities later:

> **“Did you get all that?”**

Linley immediately answered:

> **“YES.”**

Then, perhaps attempting to ensure the owl could never tell that story again, Linley asked about dwarven history instead.

Kaeporah suggested a history concerning the origins of **Fel'Garrak**.

> **“Yes. Sure. That's fine.”**

The owl departed.

Linley immediately informed everyone that he was **not** sitting through another story.

Kaeporah eventually returned carrying an old tome.

> **“Do forgive me, it is not as simple handling these items with the care they deserve, would you like for me to read—”**

> **“NO.”**

Linley would read it himself.

Payment could be deposited into the letterbox mounted on the side of the spire.

Kaeporah offered one final piece of advice:

> **“Do watch your fingers when depositing your payment.”**

The letterbox opened.

It had teeth.

Linley threw the coins into the mimic's mouth.

Needle, fascinated, approached it and spat a tremendous loogie directly inside.

The mimic swallowed.

Paused.

Then spat one back into Needle's face.

Its loogie was made of **old stamps**.

---

## The Lanternspire Inn

The party next sought accommodations.

Rather than continuing to impose upon either the Pampered Pig or Baron Horlutz, they chose the considerably more respectable-looking **Lanternspire Inn**.

Needle briefly considered finding an unoccupied room by checking the upper windows.

He knocked on one.

Then hid.

A large, extremely naked man opened it in a drunken stupor and searched fruitlessly for whoever had disturbed him.

Needle returned to the party somewhat scarred by the experience.

Inside, a shrewd-looking woman greeted them from behind a podium.

> **“Name and reservation?”**

They possessed neither.

Needle did, however, possess information.

> **“Before that, I believe one of your patrons is throwing things outside of his window? He was a large man and even more naked than I am.”**

The woman considered this.

> “Was this window on the corner facing the square?”

Yes.

She rolled her eyes.

Apparently this explained everything.

While she investigated, she invited the party to wait at the bar.

They did.

Then they saw the prices.

The enormous bartender greeted them in a sickly sweet voice wildly mismatched with the rest of him.

Needle quietly assured everyone:

**He had a plan.**

Then began ordering lavishly.

Food.

Drink.

Rations.

The bartender became sweeter with every purchase.

> **“Yes sir! Excellent choice, sir. Excellent choice! I shall have those rations prepared for you IMMEDIATELY, sir!”**


![Lanternspire.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Lanternspire.png)


Somehow, Linley understood exactly what Needle was doing.

The two exchanged knowing looks.

Soon the criminal brotherhood was operating at full capacity.

Surang and Clio wanted absolutely nothing to do with whatever was about to happen.

They left.

---

## Wandering and Home

Outside, Clio told Surang she wanted to purchase small gifts for the crew of the Pampered Pig before they left for the Glasslands.

There was no knowing how long they would be gone.

Surang finally asked her something he'd been wondering.

Why did she love the Pampered Pig so much?

Clio answered:

> **“I have spent so many years reading through texts about places I would want to see. I then spent so many years wandering to find those places. Being aboard the Pampered Pig, with its crew and traveling together like that... it's the first time I realized I could wander and still be at home.”**

Surang smiled.

>**“What of your family, Surang? Are you close to them? Seems like I’ve only ever heard you speak about Carnach.”

Surang pondered for a moment before responding.

> **“Well, yes we are close. We were all just very different I suppose. My mother was one of the village elders, so much of her time was spent serving our community. My father was a farmer which consumed most of his time. Because of that, Silence and I usually spent a lot of time with Carnach. 

>**”Tell me about your sister. The only mention you’ve made of her so far was questioning whether she attacked you in the forest…”

Surang chuckles and responds with less certainty than he likely intended.

> **“No, I don’t think so. Not after all we’ve seen in the last few days. My sister and I are twins; in that we come from the same clutch which for my people is highly unusual. She is fast and lithe in all the ways I am broad and strong. Because of her size, Carnach taught her that winning should be her first priority should she ever find herself in trouble. We were always so different but we were always there for each other.”

Clio smiles warmly

> **“I don’t have any siblings. The family member closest in age to me is my cousin, Seraphine. We are NOT close.”

Then the two went their separate ways.

Clio went searching for gifts.

Surang went to meet Tartha.

---

## Grey

At Horlutz's estate, Tartha emerged carrying three baskets.

One was hot.

Surang took them, and together they began walking toward the docks.

Along the way, Surang asked how she had come to work for the Baron.

Tartha smiled, though there was sadness in it.

> **“What do you know about Orc culture?”**

Surang admitted he knew very little.

Tartha continued.

> **“My name is Grey because of my skin tone, because of my mixed blood. From the moment I was born I did not have a place amongst my clan nor any place outside of it.”**

She kept walking.

> **“I was young, and the appetites of the men of this city would have consumed me. The Baron saved me from that terrible fate.”**

Her stride never faltered.

Neither did her voice.

> **“I'm not telling you this because I want you to pity me, mind you. I'm telling you because you deserve my honesty.”**

---

## The Golden Sun

Tartha chose their route carefully.

When a Thurandil patrol approached, she waited for it to pass.

At their first stop, just before approaching a fishmonger's wife, Tartha tapped a finger against her ear and looked at Surang.

**Listen. Don't speak.**

They set down some of the preserved pork.

The woman continued working.

> **“The sun was so golden on the horizon today.”**

Tartha glanced toward her.

> **“Was it?”**

> **“Oh, yes. Three times today I thought to myself how golden it was.”**

Tartha arranged the food.

> **“Be careful to keep your eyes averted. A golden sun is still blinding, despite being brilliant.”**

> **“Mm. Suppose you're right.”**

Nothing more was said.

Tartha motioned for Surang to follow.

---

## Green Shirts

The next destination was equally ordinary.

A laundress.

Again:

**Listen. Don't speak.**

Tartha set down another portion of food.

> **“Still getting those green shirts?”**

> **“Every third day.”**

> **“Same number?”**

> **“Near enough.”**

> **“Near enough?”**

> **“One less shirt this week. Two pairs of trousers more than usual. Nothing to fret over... yet.”**

> **“Blood?”**

> **“None. Mud, mostly. Sweat.”**

The laundress examined one of the garments.

> **“I must say though... these items are getting quite threadbare.”**

> **“Oh my. Nothing discarded yet I presume?”**

> **“Not that I've seen. Same clothes come back to me. Little worse every time.”**

Tartha nodded.

And they continued.

---

## Miss Tarfa

The final delivery brought them to a modest home on one of Il'Madris's quieter streets.

This time, Tartha gave no warning.

She simply knocked.

The door opened.

**Thuli Bramm** greeted her warmly.

> **“Oh, Tartha. Bless ye lass, well come on in out of the cold, mind you.”**

![Thuli 1.png\|500](/img/user/z_Assets/Thuli%201.png)
![BrammKids.png\|500](/img/user/z_Assets/BrammKids.png)
Then a voice exploded from inside.

> **“MISS TARFA!”**

Pip came charging into the doorway and attached himself to Tartha's leg.

> **“Careful, you little menace! This is hot!”**

Too late.

The other children appeared in short order.

Mina immediately tried to help Surang with the basket.

Nico informed everyone that Pip had been waiting at the door for twenty minutes.

Pip objected.

He had only asked whether Tartha was coming **six** times.

Elsi watched Surang shyly from behind Thuli.

And Gorrin stared upward.

And upward.

And upward.

> **“…Whoa.”**

When Tartha thanked Surang for helping carry everything, Gorrin immediately folded his arms.

> **“I could've carried it.”**

Tartha looked down.

> **“Oh, could you?”**

> “Yeah.”

> **“All of it?”**

> “…Most of it.”

> **“Of course you could, darling.”**

A quick ruffle of his hair left Gorrin immensely pleased—until he remembered Surang was watching and resumed looking tough.

Inside, Pip had climbed onto the table and stared at Surang.

> **“Are you a dwagon?”**

Gorrin immediately corrected him.

> **“He's not a dragon.”**

A pause.

> **“…He's just really big.”**

And for a while, there were no soldiers, no coded conversations, and nothing to hide.

Just Thuli Bramm, five children, Tartha, Surang, and a hot dinner.

## Ghastly

As dinner ended, Mina and Elsi approached Tartha.

They had noticed the delicate embroidery along her sleeve.

Tartha proudly told them she'd made it herself.

When the girls asked whether she could teach them, Tartha readily agreed.

Then she remembered something.

From another bag she produced a folded length of exquisite dark green Qin silk.

Mina and Elsi were mesmerized.

> **“Ooooooh!”**

Tartha grinned.

> **“I know!”**

The girls asked where she'd gotten something so beautiful.

> **“Oh, the Baron had a piece he found quite unsuitable for sale.”**

She lovingly smoothed the silk.

> **“Ghastly, apparently.”**

Elsi stared at it.

> “That?”

> **“Quite offensive to the eye, I'm told.”**

Mina gathered some of the silk into her hands, adopted her finest cantankerous expression, and began:

> **“Harumph, harumph! This is girly and dumb! Chuck it into the ocean! Harumph, harumph!”**

Elsi erupted into giggles.

So did Tartha.

> **“Now, now. If this cloth wasn't so GHASTLY, the Baron wouldn't waste it on us. So make sure you say thank you when you see him.”**

> **“Kaaaaaaaaaaaaay.”**

Pip and Nico responded appropriately.

> **“BLEEEEGH!”**

Gorrin did not.

He was staring at Tartha.

Surang noticed.

Gorrin immediately looked away.

> **“…What?”**

Nothing.

Absolutely nothing.

## The Patron Saint of Carbohydrates

Elsewhere in Il'Madris, Clio found a bakery.

Its proprietor was **Maurice**—a flamboyant, enormously muscular man apparently incapable of doing anything without tremendous enthusiasm.

![Maurice.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Maurice.png)

Clio eventually settled on a large order of beautiful rabbit-shaped muffins.

Maurice needed a little longer to finish the final batch, so Clio paid and continued shopping.

She still needed flowers.

And eventually she found them in perhaps the least impressive flower cart in Il'Madris.

The cart was run-down.

The flowers were extraordinary.

Its painfully shy proprietor approached her.

> **“M-m-may I h-help you, miss?”**

His name was **Perrin Mott**.

![Perrin Mott 1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Perrin%20Mott%201.png)

Clio selected a large bouquet of flowers that had clearly received extraordinary amounts of attention and care.

Perrin apologetically named his price.

> **“T-that'll be six silver... miss.”**

Clio gave him **five gold**.

Perrin nearly fell over.

> **“A-a-are y-y-you sure m-miss? This is far t-too much!”**

She was sure.

Perrin bowed so deeply he nearly introduced his forehead to the cobblestones.

With flowers acquired, Clio returned to the bakery.

A small goblin woman spotted her at the counter.

> **“OI MAURICE, the muffin girl is here for the goods!”**

Moments later Maurice emerged from the back—the **Patron Saint of Carbohydrates**—twirling into position with two fresh trays of hot rabbit muffins.

> **“HERE YOU ARE MY DARLING!”**

Clio was reasonably confident she would now die to preserve this man's happiness.

With flowers and baked goods in hand, she returned to the Pampered Pig to deliver her gifts to the crew.

![muffins.png](/img/user/z_Assets/muffins.png)

## What the Contract Meant

Surang and Tartha eventually returned to the Baron's estate.

Surang asked whether Horlutz was still awake.

Tartha chuckled.

> **“The old man is always awake.”**

She brought Surang inside and directed him toward the study.

Horlutz was already speaking as Surang entered.

> **“Tartha, I need for you to—”**

He looked up.

> **“...Oh it's you again. It would seem you've taken up residence inside my house, quite against my knowledge for that matter.....so what do you want?”**

Surang produced the contract.

He wanted answers.

Horlutz was incredulous.

> **“Can't you read, boy? It's all right there in black and white, what more could you possibly need to know?”**

Quite a lot, actually.

What had Carnach been transporting?

How had all of the cargo been lost?

Why had Horlutz paid him anyway?

And why, after such an apparent failure, had he hired Carnach again?

Surang had begun to wonder whether Horlutz's business dealings were perhaps less respectable than the Baron let on.

So he decided to provoke him.

> **“Seems like quite the genius business strategy. Your escort fails to protect your shipment and you pay them anyway. Moreover, you hire them again. Seems like you just enjoy throwing money away.”**

It worked.

Horlutz slammed both hands onto his desk.

> **“I CHOSE TO PAY A MAN WHO VALUED THE LIVES OF HIS MEN... NOT SOME MEANINGLESS BAUBLES.”**

Silence.

The Baron caught himself.

Then glowered at Surang.

> **“Oh I see, very clever. Very clever indeed.”**

The contract finally had its story.

> **“Well there you have it. I paid Carnach because of the loyalty he demonstrated to his crew, to keeping them alive. That isn't a quality found in a mercenary, that's the mark of an honorable man.”**

Horlutz looked at him.

> **“You tricky little shit, you really are just like him....now get out.”**

The Baron returned to the ledger in front of him.

Surang left.

And as he walked away from the estate, he found himself thinking:

> **This is a man I could serve under.**
