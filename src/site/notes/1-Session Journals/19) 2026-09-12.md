# Canon Session Recap

## The Shared Nightmare

The session began during the party’s long rest in Duskwatch.

Surang and Needle awakened together in a place made entirely of shadow. There was no visible floor, ceiling, or walls—only blackness stretching away in every direction. Unlike their previous individual nightmares, they could see, hear, and interact with one another.

A body was being dragged slowly away into the darkness.

They recognized it as **Madelay**, the refugee from the Ash Road ambush who had still been alive when the party left. Needle immediately tried to fly after her and pull her back, but no matter how he struggled he could not reach her before the darkness took her.

Then **Galtr** appeared.

![AmbushNightmare 1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/AmbushNightmare%201.png)
The enormous wound from Linley’s fatal gunshot remained open through his chest. He repeated fragments of what he had said on the Ash Road before the fighting began.

> **“No one needs to die…”**

**Tessa** followed, visibly bearing the wounds from Needle’s killing blow.

> **“Monster…”**

Shapes began moving around the edge of the dream. Wolves emerged from the darkness and slowly circled Surang and Needle.

Then they noticed something else.

A **black cat** sat perched upon Galtr’s shoulder, staring at them with glowing eyes.

The figures, the wolves, and the cat were frightening—but eventually Surang and Needle became aware that something much larger had noticed them.

The darkness itself seemed to become aware of their presence.

Surang attempted to command whatever surrounded them to **begone**, but the words failed in his throat. Needle, overwhelmed by everything around him, collapsed and became nearly catatonic.

The darkness closed in.

Both of them snapped awake.

## The Ember Escapes

Surang immediately knew that something was wrong.

Something was missing.

He checked for the black ember he had carried since destroying the True Nightmare and discovered that it was gone.

A black cat sat upon the nightstand.

The same black cat from the dream.

When Surang noticed it, the creature hissed and attempted to escape through the window.

Needle lunged forward and tried to grab it. His hands caught the creature—but its body dissolved like liquid smoke between his fingers and reformed nearby.

Surang brought his hammer down and attempted to destroy it with a smite.

The creature escaped again.

The commotion woke Clio and Linley just as the cat darted toward the door.

Before it could escape, **Clio’s bag opened on its own**.

Her map pulled itself free.

The paper folded and creased until it had transformed into a small paper doll.

The doll intercepted the creature.

> **“Oh, no you don’t.”**

Then, with considerably more familiarity than anyone expected:

> **“Come here, Petal.”**


![DollvsCat 1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/DollvsCat%201.png)

The paper figure caught the thing and forced it back into the form of the black ember.

Through the paper doll, the party finally received a proper introduction to **Auntie Esther**.

Esther made it very clear that she had been watching their handling of the object with growing irritation. They had already been warned not to sleep beside it, had continued carrying it through multiple nights of increasingly violent dreams, and had now allowed it to become something capable of **growing legs and attempting to leave**.

When the party questioned the relationship between the ember and their nightmares, Esther made clear that it was responsible for enough to be dangerous and had apparently been **feeding**.

Questions about the far greater presence they had encountered in the dream produced a markedly different response.

The doll became still.

The sarcasm disappeared.

> **“…Bring me the ember.”**

Pressed further, Esther refused to discuss the matter remotely.

> **“Not here.”**

She instructed them to bring the object to her.

The map subsequently marked **Bogwatch Bothy** and provided a suggested route through the Somn, directing them toward the river.

The doll then folded back into the map.

## Sealing the Ember

With the ember returned to them, Surang and Linley decided that carrying it loosely was no longer acceptable.

They retrieved the **Hamyrhal tea box**.

The ember was placed inside, and when the two halves of the box were brought together, the seam vanished entirely.

Linley immediately argued that the safest solution was simpler:

**throw it into the ocean.**

Surang was not entirely opposed. He was deeply uncomfortable both with continuing to carry the thing and with simply delivering such a dangerous object into Esther’s hands.

The rest of the group was considerably less willing to dispose of it.

For the moment, the ember remained sealed inside the tea box.

## First Mate Madagan

Only moments later came a knock at the door.

Madagan stuck his head inside.

> **“Good morning sailors! Sleep well?!”**

The party made their way down to the docks, where Madagan led them to one of the largest ships presently berthed there.

He proudly presented their transportation.

> **“Welcome to the Pampered Pig!”**

![PPNice1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/PPNice1.png)
![PPNice2.png](/img/user/z_Assets/PPNice2.png)
The deck was already alive with sailors preparing to depart, with Madagan enthusiastically directing the work.

One sailor answered an order:

> **“Yes, Cap’n!”**

Madagan immediately erupted.

> **“I’LL THROW YOU OVERBOARD!”**

The sailor corrected himself.

> **“Yes, First Mate!”**

And the **Pampered Pig** departed Duskwatch.

## Life Aboard the Pampered Pig

The party adapted to life at sea with wildly different levels of enthusiasm.

**Clio and Needle loved it almost immediately.**

![Pirates1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Pirates1.png)
![Pirates2.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Pirates2.png)

The sailors, the shouting, the ropes, the sea shanties, and the general atmosphere of something suspiciously close to piracy suited both of them perfectly.

The crew continued insisting:

> **“We’re not pirates…?”**

Surang proved somewhat unsteady but otherwise managed without serious difficulty.

**Linley was miserable.**

The movement of the ship immediately disagreed with him, and he spent the early voyage suffering badly until Madagan supplied him with **banana rum**, which provided at least some degree of normalcy.

Needle quickly appointed himself an unofficial officer.

Unfortunately, most of his commands consisted of ordering sailors to perform tasks they were already visibly performing.

> **“Mop the deck, sailor!!”**

Directed, naturally, at a man currently holding a mop.

Clio began talking with the sailors about the ship, Madagan, their employer, and the ship’s former captain. The crew were more than happy to speak with her.

As the journey continued—and especially once Clio began contributing music to the already shanty-heavy voyage—the crew became increasingly enamored with both her and her performances.

![ClioOnThePamperedPig.png](/img/user/z_Assets/ClioOnThePamperedPig.png)

## Madagan’s Captain

Surang eventually approached Madagan about **Carnach**.

Madagan was delighted.

He happily supplied Surang with several stories about his former captain, although the stories rapidly demonstrated that Madagan’s recollections should not necessarily be interpreted literally.

Carnach had apparently accomplished feats of increasingly improbable heroism across the seas.

Somewhere beneath the exaggeration, however, Madagan’s affection and admiration for his captain were unmistakable.

Surang told Madagan directly that **he knew Carnach**.

He did not explain how.

More importantly, Surang eventually chose **not to tell Madagan that Carnach was dead**.

As far as Madagan knows, his captain left his life aboard the Pampered Pig years ago.

What happened afterward remains unknown to him.

## Needle Discovers Lantern Oil

At another point during the voyage, Needle asked Madagan where the ship kept its:

> **“Booze.”**

Needle followed him briefly before becoming distracted by the first available barrel.

He filled a tankard from it and flew triumphantly toward the crow’s nest.

Madagan stared after him.

Then he turned toward Surang.

> **“Your baby can drink lantern oil??”**

Against all reasonable expectations, Needle proceeded to prove that **yes, apparently he can**.

A natural 20 ensured that there were no immediate consequences for consuming a tankard of lantern oil.

## Linley and the Ember

As night approached, Linley returned to the matter of the nightmare ember.

He confronted Clio and asked why she still had not agreed to throw it into the sea.

Clio attempted to reason with him for some time, but the conversation eventually reached an impasse.

She told Linley plainly that she was **not throwing the ember into the ocean**, and that the discussion was over.

At that moment, a tiny paper figure appeared at the corner of Clio’s bag.

Esther’s paper doll looked directly at Linley.

It gave him the middle finger.

Then it disappeared back inside the bag.

## Carnach’s Quarters

Later that evening, Madagan brought Surang to the captain’s quarters.

Before entering, Madagan **knocked**.

The room remained largely intact, apparently preserved much as Carnach had left it.

His belongings were still there.

Among them was a map recording the oceans, coasts, and distant lands that the **Pampered Pig** had traveled during Carnach’s years aboard her.

For Surang, it was a glimpse into a part of his mentor’s life that he had never known.

Carnach had existed before Scales.

Before Surang.

And the room remained as evidence of the man Madagan had known as **Captain Carnach**.

## Tavern Brawler

Needle’s appreciation of shipboard culture eventually manifested in another form.

While waiting in line for food, he deliberately started a fight with one of the sailors.

The altercation escalated until Needle knocked the sailor completely unconscious.

In doing so, Needle fulfilled the remaining requirements for **Tavern Brawler**.

## Giant’s Grasp

The following morning, Lowshield appeared ahead.

From the sea, the party could see the enormous stone structures protecting the harbor: the ancient barrier known as **Giant’s Grasp**.

The great stones broke the force of the sea and created the sheltered waters from which Lowshield derives its identity.

As the Pampered Pig approached the narrow passage into port, the party joined the crew in helping guide the vessel through.

They rowed and assisted with the lines while the sailors worked the ship through the channel between the ancient stones.

Before this could happen, however, Madagan first had to break up what was rapidly becoming **half of the crew assembling around Clio**.

He ordered the sailors back to work.

The Pampered Pig passed through Giant’s Grasp and entered Lowshield.

![Lowshield 1.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Lowshield%201.png)
## The Sheltered Tide

Madagan came ashore with the party and immediately insisted that they try one of Lowshield’s local specialties:

**breakwater cakes**.

Several members of the crew attempted to follow Clio ashore.

Madagan turned them around and sent them back to watch the ship.

Clio stopped first at a local shop and purchased several goods before eventually rejoining the others.

The group gathered at **The Sheltered Tide**.

The building was considerably larger than an ordinary tavern and clearly served as one of Lowshield’s primary communal gathering places.

Its proprietor, **Bram**, welcomed the group.

Madagan’s recommendation proved justified: Bram provided the party with Lowshield’s famous breakwater cakes, as well as additional supplies purchased by Clio and Linley.

## Needle’s Headaches

Throughout their time in Lowshield, Needle had been suffering from increasingly severe headaches.

At first, the obvious explanation seemed to be his earlier fight aboard the Pampered Pig.

As time passed, however, the pain continued.

Something about Lowshield was affecting him.

Bram eventually began explaining the history and legend of the settlement.

He told the party the story of the giants who once lived there and the events surrounding the creation of Lowshield and Giant’s Grasp.

As Bram reached the end of the legend, Needle’s condition abruptly worsened.

He collapsed.

Unconscious.

The party immediately attended to him.

## The Storm

With Needle recovering, the group began preparing to say goodbye to Madagan and the crew of the Pampered Pig.

Then thunder cracked overhead.

A storm was moving in.

Madagan looked toward the worsening weather.

> **“Well I guess we’re staying too!”**

The Pampered Pig would not be departing Lowshield that evening.

Surang used the opportunity to ask Bram more about **Giant’s Grasp**, particularly whether people ever climbed the enormous stones.

Bram explained that there was generally little reason to do so.

Unless, of course, someone wanted to visit the old **Giants’ shrine** located atop one of the pillars.

With the storm arriving, Needle suffering from unexplained symptoms, and the location of the shrine now known, the party decided to stop for the night.
